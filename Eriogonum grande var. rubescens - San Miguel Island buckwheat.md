San Miguel Island buckwheat (_Eriogonum grande_ var. _rubescens_) is a low evergreen sub-shrub endemic to California's Channel Islands, grown for its showy red to pink flowers. It grows 0.2-0.5 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
