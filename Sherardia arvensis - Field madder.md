Field madder (_Sherardia arvensis_) is an annual herb of most of [[Europe]] to the Mediterranean. It does well in hot, dry, sunny positions where it self-sows, and can become a lawn weed. A red or rose dye is obtained from the plant.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Dye]][^1]
