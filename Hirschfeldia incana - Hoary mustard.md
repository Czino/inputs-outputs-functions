Hoary mustard (_Hirschfeldia incana_) is an annual herb native to the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] region and naturalized widely elsewhere as a weed of disturbed and cultivated ground.[^1] Its young leaves are eaten raw and its seeds, raw, cooked, or ground into a powder, are used as food; in parts of Greece the young plant is eaten dressed with oil and lemon juice.[^1] It grows up to 1 m tall.[^1]
