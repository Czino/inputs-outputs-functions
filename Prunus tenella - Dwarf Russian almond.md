Dwarf Russian almond (_Prunus tenella_) is a cold-hardy deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] dry grassland and steppe from [[Europe|south-eastern Europe]] to [[Asia|Central Asia and Tibet]], grown for its fruit and almond-scented seed oil.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Dye]][^1]
