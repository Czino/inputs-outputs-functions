Fendler's ceanothus (_Ceanothus fendleri_) is a deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] dry slopes and open woodland across the [[North America|North American]] Southwest, its berries eaten as food and its leaves used as a tea substitute.
