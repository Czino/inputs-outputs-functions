Nitre bush (_Nitraria schoberi_) is a drought-tolerant, salt-tolerant shrub of saline desert and [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe soils across [[Asia]], grown for its small, sweet-salty edible fruit.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
