Clustered field sedge (_Carex praegracilis_) is a rhizomatous, mat-forming perennial sedge of wet meadows, marshes, and seasonally flooded ground across [[North America|western North America]], handling standing water and grazed as livestock forage.[^1] It grows 0.1-0.5 m tall and 0.1-1 m wide.[^1]
