Pichi (_Fabiana imbricata_) is an evergreen shrub of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] Andean foothills and Patagonian steppe of [[South America|Chile]], its dried leaves and twigs used as a traditional diuretic and urinary tonic.
