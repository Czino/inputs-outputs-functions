Common catchfly (_Silene gallica_) is an annual herb of central and southern [[Europe]]. Its juice has a folk-medicinal use as an ophthalmic remedy, and the plant is said to be emollient.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

- [[Annual]]
