Grape soda lupine (_Lupinus excubitus_) is a small, evergreen bush lupine ranging from California desert to mountain habitats, with violet flowers fragrant enough to smell like grape soda. It grows 0.5-1.5 m tall and 1-1.5 m wide.[^1]
