Thyme-leaved sandwort (_Arenaria serpyllifolia_) is a small annual or biennial herb of bare ground, walls and chalk downs across most of [[Europe]], temperate [[Asia]] and [[North America]], and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] The entire plant is used as a pot-herb, and it has a history of use as an antitussive and diuretic remedy.[^1]
