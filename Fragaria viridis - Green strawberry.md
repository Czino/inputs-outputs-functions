Green strawberry (_Fragaria viridis_) is a perennial herb of [[Europe]] and a constant species of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] It bears sweet, musky-flavoured fruit and spreads by surface stolons into a dense carpet.[^1]
