Lemonade berry (_Rhus integrifolia_) is an evergreen shrub native to coastal Southern California, in [[North America]], its tart fruit soaked in water to make a lemonade-like drink.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Rhus spp. - Sumac]]
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
