Skull cap (_Scutellaria austinae_, syn. _Scutellaria siphocampyloides_) is a small evergreen perennial forming a foot-wide clump of deep royal blue flowers, native to California mountain forests up to 6000-7000 feet. It grows 0.1-0.2 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
