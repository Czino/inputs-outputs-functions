Mediterranean coriaria (_Coriaria myrtifolia_), also called redoul, is a deciduous, nitrogen-fixing shrub native to the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] region (Spain, France, Italy, Greece and North Africa), growing in dry woods, hedges and rocky ground.[^1] All parts of the plant are very poisonous, with effects likened to alcoholic intoxication, and it has no food or medicinal use.[^1]
