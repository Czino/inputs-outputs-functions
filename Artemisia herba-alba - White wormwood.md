White wormwood (_Artemisia herba-alba_) is a small, aromatic evergreen shrub of dry steppe and desert soils across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] interior [[Europe|Spain]] and North Africa, long used as a herbal remedy and fuel plant. Skin contact with some Artemisia species can cause dermatitis in sensitive people.
