Wood's rose (_Rosa woodsii_) is a deciduous, thicket-forming shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] plains and foothills, its hips and flowers used for food and its bark and root for traditional medicine.
