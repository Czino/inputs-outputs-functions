Headed savory (_Coridothymus capitatus_, synonym _Thymbra capitata_) is an evergreen shrub of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]], growing on dry, usually calcareous soils. Its oregano-scented leaves and essential oil ("Spanish oregano oil") are used fresh or dried as a culinary flavoring, and the oil is a strong antiseptic and disinfectant.[^1]
