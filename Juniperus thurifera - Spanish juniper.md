Spanish juniper (_Juniperus thurifera_) is a slow-growing evergreen conifer of the semi-arid woodlands (sabinares) of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] interior [[Europe|Spain]] and the Atlas Mountains, typically found on calcareous, rocky soils. It is a natural pioneer species, colonizing open and abandoned arable land.
