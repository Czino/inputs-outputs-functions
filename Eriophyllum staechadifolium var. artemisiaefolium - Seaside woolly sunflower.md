Seaside woolly sunflower (_Eriophyllum staechadifolium_ var. _artemisiaefolium_) is a flat to mounding evergreen perennial of California's coastal bluffs, grown as a nectar plant for native bees and butterflies. It grows 0.5-0.8 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
