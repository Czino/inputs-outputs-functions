Cretan bryony (_Bryonia cretica_) is a deciduous perennial climbing vine of scrub and woodland across central and southern [[Europe]], extending to [[North Africa]] and West Asia.[^1] Its young shoots and leaves are cooked and eaten in spring, though all other parts of the plant are poisonous and its fresh root is a severe skin irritant.[^1] It grows up to 3.5 m tall and 3 m wide.[^1]
