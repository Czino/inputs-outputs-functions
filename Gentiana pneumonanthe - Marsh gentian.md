Marsh gentian (_Gentiana pneumonanthe_) is a perennial herb of boggy meadows, fens, and moist heaths across [[Europe]], from Scandinavia south to Spain and Macedonia and east to the Caucasus and Siberia; it is now very local and declining in parts of its range.[^1]

- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
