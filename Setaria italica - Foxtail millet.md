Foxtail millet (_Setaria italica_) is a fast-maturing, drought-tolerant annual grain crop of continental, [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[Asia]], among the oldest cultivated cereals and a staple food in dry, short-season climates.
