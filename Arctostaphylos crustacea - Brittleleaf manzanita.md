Brittleleaf manzanita (_Arctostaphylos crustacea_) is a slow-growing evergreen shrub of California's central coastal chaparral, with a basal burl, reddish stems, and small bright-pink flowers. It grows 1-1.5 m tall and 0.5-2 m wide.[^1] Its fruit is edible.[^1]
