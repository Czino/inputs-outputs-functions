Brittle pricklypear (_Opuntia fragilis_) is the most cold-hardy North American cactus, a low, mat-forming [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|prairie and plains]] species with edible pads and fruit.

- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
