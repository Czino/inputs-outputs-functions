Mayten (_Maytenus boaria_) is an evergreen tree of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] Patagonian pastureland in [[South America|Argentina and Chile]], its seed pressed for a cooking oil.

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
