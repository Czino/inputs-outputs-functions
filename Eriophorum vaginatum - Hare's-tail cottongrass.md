Hare's-tail cottongrass (_Eriophorum vaginatum_) is a tussock-forming sedge of permanently wet, peaty acid bogs and moorland, native to Great Britain and Ireland. Its cotton-like seedheads make it a plant for pond edges and bog gardens.[^1]
