Golden fleece (_Ericameria arborescens_) is a fast-growing, stress-deciduous shrub of California chaparral, yellow pine forest, and oak woodland, useful for quick color and site stabilization until longer-lived trees establish.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and wide.[^1]
