Chaparral mallow (_Malacothamnus fasciculatus_) is an evergreen shrub native to southern California chaparral and coastal sage scrub, grown as a hedge plant for its gray-green foliage and pink summer flowers. It grows 1-3 m tall and wide.[^1]
