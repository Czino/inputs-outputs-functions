Dwarf wild indigo (_Amorpha nana_) is a low, nitrogen-fixing deciduous shrub of dry, [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairies, its deep, spreading roots useful for erosion control.
