Hairy stemmed rhipsalis (_Rhipsalis pilocarpa_) is an evergreen epiphytic cactus native to rainforests of southeastern [[South America|Brazil]] (Minas Gerais, Espírito Santo, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo and Paraná), growing at 500-900 m elevation and known from only a handful of localities.[^1] Its slender, cylindrical stems are covered in fine white hairs, unusual for the genus, and form pendent bushes grown as a houseplant.[^1] It reaches 0.1-0.5 m tall and wide.[^2]
