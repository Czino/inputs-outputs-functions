Stream orchid (_Epipactis gigantea_) is a deciduous perennial orchid of wet and moist places across California, with yellow-brown flowers veined in purple, going dormant at the first sign of drought.[^1] It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 0.7-2 m wide.[^1]
