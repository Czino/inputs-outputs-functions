Goldenrod (_Solidago virgaurea_) is a perennial herb of most of [[Europe]], temperate [[Asia]] and [[North America]]. Its leaves are used for tea, it has an extensive history of folk-medicinal use, and dyes are obtained from the whole plant.[^1]
