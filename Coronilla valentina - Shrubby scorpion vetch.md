Shrubby scorpion vetch (_Coronilla valentina_) is an evergreen shrub native to the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] Basin and southern Portugal, grown for its fragrant, bright yellow flowers that appear in late winter and early spring, with a second flush in late summer.[^1]
