Mexican petunia (_Ruellia simplex_) is a herbaceous perennial native to Mexico, Central and South America and the Caribbean, grown as an ornamental for its lavender-to-violet, petunia-like flowers, each of which opens for only one day. It reaches 0.6-1.2 m tall and 0.6-0.9 m wide.[^1] It spreads aggressively by rhizomes and self-seeding and is listed as a Category One invasive species in Florida, where it has naturalized widely.[^1]
