Hoary-leaved ceanothus (_Ceanothus crassifolius_) is a drought-resistant evergreen shrub or small tree native to the chaparral of Southern California's interior mountains, growing in full sun on clay-loam soils.[^1] It grows 2-4 m tall and wide.[^1]
