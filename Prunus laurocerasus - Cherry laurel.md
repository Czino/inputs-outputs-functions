Cherry laurel (Prunus laurocerasus) is an evergreen shrub grown for its dense, glossy foliage, widely used as a clipped hedge and screening plant. The fully ripe fruit is edible and leaves can be distilled for an almond-like flavoring, but all parts of the plant contain cyanogenic compounds and bitter fruit or seed should not be eaten.
