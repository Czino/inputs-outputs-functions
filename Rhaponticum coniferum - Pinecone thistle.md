Pinecone thistle (_Rhaponticum coniferum_, synonym _Leuzea conifera_) is a clump-forming perennial herb of pine woodland and rosemary-thyme garrigue across the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]], named for its flower head, which resembles a small pinecone.[^1] It grows 0.1-0.5 m tall and wide.[^2]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^2]
- [[Well drained soil]][^2]

- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]
