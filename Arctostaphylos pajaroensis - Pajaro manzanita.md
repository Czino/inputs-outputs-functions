Pajaro manzanita (_Arctostaphylos pajaroensis_) is a sprawling evergreen shrub native to the Pajaro Hills area of California's central coast, grown as a ground cover or border planting for its dark red bark and bright red new growth.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and wide.[^1]
