St. Catherine's lace (_Eriogonum giganteum_) is an evergreen shrub endemic to California's Channel Islands, grown as a background plant for its gray foliage and large butterfly-attracting flower heads. It grows 0.5-1.5 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
