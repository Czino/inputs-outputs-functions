Hillside gooseberry (_Ribes californicum_) is a deciduous shrub native to California, in [[North America]], its fruit edible raw, cooked, dried, or made into preserves.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 6]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

- [[Deciduous]]
