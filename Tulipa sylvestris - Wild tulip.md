Wild tulip (_Tulipa sylvestris_) is a bulb native to [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]], naturalized in meadows, orchards and rocky places further north. Its bulb and flowers can cause dermatitis in sensitive people.[^1]
