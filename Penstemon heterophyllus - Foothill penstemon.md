Foothill penstemon (_Penstemon heterophyllus_) is a long-lived perennial of dry coast-range hills in California, bearing hundreds of violet, inch-long flowers on three-foot spikes. It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 0.5-1.5 m wide.[^1]
