White bladderflower (_Araujia sericifera_) is a fast-growing, evergreen climbing vine native to [[South America]], naturalized on sandy coasts and waste ground in [[Europe|southwestern Europe]].[^1] Its strongly scented flowers are pollinated by moths, and a durable fibre can be stripped from its stems.[^1] It climbs up to 10 m.[^1]

- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
