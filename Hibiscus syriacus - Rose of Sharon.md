Rose of Sharon (_Hibiscus syriacus_) is a deciduous shrub native to eastern [[Asia]] and locally naturalized in southern [[Europe]], where it is planted as a hedge. Its young leaves and flowers are eaten raw or cooked, and the leaves and flowers make a tea.
