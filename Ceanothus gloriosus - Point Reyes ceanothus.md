Point Reyes ceanothus (_Ceanothus gloriosus_) is a fast-spreading evergreen groundcover ceanothus of the Northern California coast, with blue flowers on dark green foliage, among the best choices for stabilizing beach sand.[^1] It grows 0.2-0.3 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
