Round-leaved sundew (_Drosera rotundifolia_) is an evergreen perennial of wet, nutrient-poor peaty ground across [[Europe]], northern Asia, and North America, sometimes forming a floating fringe on small ponds. It is carnivorous, trapping insects on sticky leaf hairs to obtain nutrients that its soil cannot supply.[^1]
