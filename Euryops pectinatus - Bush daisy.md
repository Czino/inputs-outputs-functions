Grey-leaved euryops (_Euryops pectinatus_), also sold as bush daisy, is an evergreen shrub native to [[South Africa]], grown for its grey-green foliage and yellow daisy-like flowers that bloom from early summer into winter under cover.[^1] It grows 0.5-1 m tall and wide.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]
