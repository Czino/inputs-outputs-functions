Knobcone pine (_Pinus attenuata_) is a small evergreen tree of California's closed-cone pine forests, usually staying 15 to 20 feet tall, that struggles alone but grows well in groves with oaks, pines, cottonwoods, or firs.[^1] It grows 5-15 m tall and 5-10 m wide.[^1]
