Elephant's ear (_Colocasia esculenta_), also called taro, is an evergreen perennial herb grown from a corm, probably native to tropical [[Asia]] and widely cultivated across the tropics.[^1] Its starchy corm is cooked like a potato and its young leaves and stems are cooked as a vegetable; all parts contain calcium oxalate crystals and must be thoroughly cooked, as they are toxic raw.[^1] It grows up to 1 m tall and about 1 m wide.[^1]
