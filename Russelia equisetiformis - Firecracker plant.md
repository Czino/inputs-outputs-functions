Firecracker plant (_Russelia equisetiformis_) is a perennial shrub native to [[North America|Mexico]], grown for its arching, nearly leafless stems and tubular red flowers.[^1] It grows 0.6-1.2 m tall and 0.9-1.5 m wide.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
