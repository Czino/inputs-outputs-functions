Yawning penstemon (_Keckiella breviflora_) is a sprawling, stress-deciduous subshrub of California foothill and mountain woodlands, leaning on rocks or neighboring plants and dropping its leaves under summer drought. It grows 0.7-1.5 m tall and 1-1.5 m wide.[^1]
