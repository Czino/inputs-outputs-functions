Pacific madrone (_Arbutus menziesii_) is an evergreen tree native to the Pacific coast of [[North America]], its red fruit edible raw or cooked and its bark used as an astringent and a dye.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Dye]][^1]
- [[Wood]][^1]

## Type
- [[Tree]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
