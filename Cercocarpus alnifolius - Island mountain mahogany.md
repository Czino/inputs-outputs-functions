Island mountain mahogany (_Cercocarpus alnifolius_) is a nitrogen-fixing evergreen shrub that looks like a compact birch tree, tolerating clay soil and years of neglect once established.[^1] Deer readily browse it, so it fares poorly where deer are abundant.[^1] It grows 2-5 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
