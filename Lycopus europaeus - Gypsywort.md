Gypsywort (_Lycopus europaeus_) is a perennial herb of riverbanks, ditches, marshes, and fens across [[Europe]], the Mediterranean, and north and central [[Asia]]. Its root is edible but only as an emergency food — a famine food used only when all else fails.[^1]
