Sheep's fescue (_Festuca ovina_) is a densely tufted perennial grass native to temperate [[Europe]], [[Asia]], and [[North Africa]], favouring poor, dry, well-drained soils; its small seed has been gathered as an emergency food in times of famine, not a regular one.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Emergency food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
