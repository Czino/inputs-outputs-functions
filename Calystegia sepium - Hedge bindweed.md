Hedge bindweed (_Calystegia sepium_) is a vigorous perennial climbing vine native to [[Europe]], [[Asia]], and [[North America]], found in hedges and woodland edges. Its root and young shoots are edible but should not be eaten regularly, since they have a possible purgative effect.[^1]
