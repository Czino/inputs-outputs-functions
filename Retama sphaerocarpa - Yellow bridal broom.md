Yellow bridal broom (_Retama sphaerocarpa_) is a leafless, deciduous shrub of arid, sandy soils across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] interior [[Europe|Spain]] and [[North Africa]], its green stems carrying out photosynthesis through most of the year. As a legume, it fixes nitrogen through root-nodule symbiosis with soil bacteria. A strong decoction of the plant is used medicinally but can cause vomiting and headaches.
