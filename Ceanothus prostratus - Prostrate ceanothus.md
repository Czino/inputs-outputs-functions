Prostrate ceanothus (_Ceanothus prostratus_) is a mat-forming, nitrogen-fixing evergreen shrub native to California's montane chaparral, in western [[North America]], its shallow, spreading roots forming a flat ground-covering mat barely 10 cm tall.[^1]
