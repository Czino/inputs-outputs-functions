Dyer's litmus (_Chrozophora tinctoria_) is a perennial herb of dry, coastal ground around the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] and across [[Europe]] into western and central Asia and [[North Africa]]. Red and blue dyes are extracted from its flowers, fruit, and sap.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]
