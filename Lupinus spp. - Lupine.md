Lupinus is a genus of legumes with palmate leaves and tall flower spikes, grown for nitrogen fixation, ornamental value, and, in some species, edible seeds.

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 6]][^3]
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^3]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^3]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^3]
- [[Full sun]][^3]
- [[Moist soil]][^3]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^3]
- [[Medicinal use]][^3]

## Type
- [[Herb]]

## Functions
- [[Phosphorus accumulator]][^1]
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^2]
