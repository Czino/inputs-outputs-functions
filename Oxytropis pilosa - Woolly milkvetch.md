Woolly milkvetch (_Oxytropis pilosa_) is a perennial herb native to [[Europe]], from Sweden to the French Alps, east through Russia, the Caucasus and northern Turkey to Siberia, Kazakhstan and western China, growing in valleys, hilly areas, meadows and shrublands.
