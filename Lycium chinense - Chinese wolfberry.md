Chinese wolfberry (_Lycium chinense_) is a deciduous, spiny shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and warm-temperate [[Asia|China]], its sweet berries, leaves, and root bark all used as food and in traditional Chinese medicine.
