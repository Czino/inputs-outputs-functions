Common milkweed (_Asclepias syriaca_) is a perennial herb of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] thickets, roadsides, and dry fields across eastern [[North America]], with edible young shoots and flower buds and a long history of medicinal use.
