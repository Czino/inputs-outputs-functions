Saxaul (_Haloxylon ammodendron_) is an evergreen shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] sand-desert steppe across [[Asia|Central Asia, Mongolia, and northern China]], planted extensively to stabilize shifting desert sand.

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Dye]][^1]
- [[Fuel]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
