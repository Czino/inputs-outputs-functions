Wild angelica (_Angelica sylvestris_) is a biennial herb native to most of [[Europe]] and W. [[Asia]], found in moist fields, hedgerows, and open woods. Like other members of its genus, it contains furocoumarins that can increase skin sensitivity to sunlight and cause dermatitis on contact.[^1]
