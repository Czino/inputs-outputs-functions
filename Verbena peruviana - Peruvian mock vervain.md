Peruvian mock vervain (Verbena peruviana) is a low-growing perennial with vibrant red, pink, or purple flowers. It is often used as ground cover and in hanging baskets for its long blooming period and ability to attract butterflies.
## Type
- [[Verbena spp. - Verbena]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Attracting beneficial insects]][^1]
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
