Salad burnet (_Sanguisorba minor_) is an evergreen perennial herb of calcareous grassland across [[Europe]], and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] Its young leaves, mildly cucumber-flavoured, are available fresh through the winter.[^1]
