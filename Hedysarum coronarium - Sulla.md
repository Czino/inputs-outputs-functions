Sulla (_Hedysarum coronarium_) is a biennial or short-lived perennial forage legume native to the western [[Mediterranean]] and North Africa, widely grown across the [[Mediterranean]] basin for grazing, hay, and silage. As a nitrogen fixer it also serves as a soil-building pioneer species on degraded ground.
