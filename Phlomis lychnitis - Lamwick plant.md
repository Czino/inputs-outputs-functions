Lamwick plant (_Phlomis lychnitis_) is a deciduous shrub native to south-western [[Europe]], found on rocky ground and [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] garrigue, mainly on calcareous soils.[^1] Its leaves are occasionally used to adulterate sage.
