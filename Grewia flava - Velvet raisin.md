Velvet raisin (_Grewia flava_) is a compact, deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] dry woodland and bushland across southern [[Africa]], its fruit widely eaten fresh or dried and its mucilaginous leaves, stems, and roots used medicinally.
