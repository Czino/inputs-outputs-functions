Cuckoo pint (_Arum maculatum_) is a woodland perennial herb native to [[Europe|Europe]] and [[North Africa|North Africa]]. Its tuber and leaves are edible once thoroughly dried or cooked — raw, the plant contains calcium oxalate crystals that cause intense burning in the mouth and tongue.[^1]
