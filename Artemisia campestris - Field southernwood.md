Field southernwood (_Artemisia campestris_) is a perennial herb of temperate regions of the northern hemisphere and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] It has milder medicinal properties than the related southernwood.[^1]
