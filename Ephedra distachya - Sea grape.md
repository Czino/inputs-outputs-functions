Sea grape (_Ephedra distachya_) is an evergreen shrub of dune, steppe, and rocky habitats across southern [[Europe]], including [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[Europe|Spain]], east to central [[Asia]].[^2] It is a source of the alkaloid ephedrine, used traditionally to treat asthma and other respiratory complaints, though it should only be used under professional medical supervision.
