Chinese juniper (_Juniperus chinensis_) is a slow-growing evergreen conifer native to [[Asia|East Asia]] (China, Japan, Mongolia, Korea and the Russian Far East), growing naturally near seashores in central and southern Japan.[^1] Various parts are used in traditional medicine for skin complaints and rheumatism, and low, spreading cultivars are planted as ground cover.[^1] It grows up to 15 m tall and 3 m wide.[^1]
