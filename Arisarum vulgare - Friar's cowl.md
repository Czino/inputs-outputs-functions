Friar's cowl (_Arisarum vulgare_) is a shade-tolerant perennial herb of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]]. Its tuber-like root, about the size of a walnut, was used as an emergency food in times of scarcity once thoroughly washed and cooked — raw, it contains calcium oxalate crystals that cause intense burning in the mouth.[^1]
