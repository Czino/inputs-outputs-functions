California buckwheat (_Eriogonum fasciculatum_ var. _foliolosum_) is an evergreen shrub or sub-shrub of California chaparral, coastal sage scrub and creosote bush scrub, its flowers, leaves and seeds feeding a dense web of native bees, butterflies and small birds. It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
