Spindle tree (_Euonymus europaeus_) is a deciduous shrub native to [[Europe|Europe]]. All parts of the plant are poisonous, so despite PFAF listing a dye obtained from the fruit and seed under "Edible Uses," it is not treated here as a food plant.[^1]
