Blue-eyed grass (_Sisyrinchium bellum_) is a small, grass-leaved perennial of California grassy slopes and redwood forest understory, widely distributed from coastal bluffs to interior grasslands, with blue iris-like flowers from January to June. It grows 0.3-0.7 m tall and 0.1-0.5 m wide.[^1]
