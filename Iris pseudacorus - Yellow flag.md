Yellow flag (_Iris pseudacorus_) is a perennial herb of marshes, ditches, and shallow water across [[Europe]], [[Asia]], and [[North Africa]]. All parts, especially the rhizome, contain an acrid irritant resin, and the seeds are edible only when well roasted — raw seeds or root can cause vomiting and diarrhoea.[^1]
