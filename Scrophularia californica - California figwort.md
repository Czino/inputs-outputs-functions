California figwort (_Scrophularia californica_) is an evergreen perennial of California coastal woodlands and chaparral, growing from Los Angeles to British Columbia. Its iridoid glycosides make the plant unpalatable, so it is not eaten. It grows 1-1.5 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
