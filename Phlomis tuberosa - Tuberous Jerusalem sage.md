Tuberous Jerusalem sage (_Phlomis tuberosa_) is a cold-hardy perennial herb with starchy edible tubers, native from Eastern Europe through the Caucasus and Siberia to Mongolia and northern China.[^1] Its dry, rocky steppe habitat and tolerance of temperatures down to -25°C make it a fit for [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] climates.[^1]
