Sand cherry (_Prunus pumila_) is a low, deciduous shrub native to [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] sand plains and dunes, the largest-fruited of the native American cherries. Like other _Prunus_ species, its seed contains cyanide-forming compounds and should not be eaten if bitter.
