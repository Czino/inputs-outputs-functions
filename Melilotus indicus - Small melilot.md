Small melilot (_Melilotus indicus_) is a fast-growing, nitrogen-fixing annual herb native to [[Europe]] (Mediterranean to E. Asia), temperate and tropical [[Asia]], and parts of [[Africa]], and naturalized widely elsewhere.[^1] Its young leaves and flowers are eaten, and its seed gruel is used medicinally, though mouldy or dried plant material can contain toxic anticoagulant compounds.[^1] It grows up to 0.7 m tall and 0.6 m wide.[^1]
