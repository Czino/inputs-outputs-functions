Wood mint (_Stachys bullata_) is a perennial herb native to California, in western [[North America]], its leaves used traditionally as a disinfectant wash and poultice for wounds, earaches, and sore throats.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
