Wood sanicle (_Sanicula europaea_) is a perennial herb native to [[Europe]], [[North Africa]], and temperate [[Asia]], found in woods and damp, shaded places; its saponin-containing leaves and young shoots have been eaten cooked only as an emergency food in times of want, not a regular one.[^1]
