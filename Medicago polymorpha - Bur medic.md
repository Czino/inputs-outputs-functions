Bur medic (_Medicago polymorpha_) is an annual herb grown as a nitrogen-fixing green manure, with edible young leaves, flowers, and seed.

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
