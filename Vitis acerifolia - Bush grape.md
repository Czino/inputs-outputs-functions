Bush grape (_Vitis acerifolia_) is a cold-hardy deciduous vine of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] stream bottoms and rocky slopes across the southern [[North America|Great Plains]], its sweet fruit eaten fresh or dried.
