Star jasmine (_Trachelospermum jasminoides_) is a slow-growing, fragrant evergreen climber native to eastern [[Asia]] (China, Japan, Korea and Taiwan), grown for its scented white flowers.[^1] It grows up to 6 m tall and 6 m wide.[^1]
