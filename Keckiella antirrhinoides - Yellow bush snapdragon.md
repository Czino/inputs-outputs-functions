Yellow bush snapdragon (_Keckiella antirrhinoides_), also called yellow bush penstemon, is a drought-deciduous perennial shrub of southern California's interior chaparral, grown for its fragrant red-yellow flowers and value to hummingbirds. It grows 1-2 m tall and 0.7-1 m wide.[^1]
