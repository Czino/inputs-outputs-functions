The [USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map](https://planthardiness.ars.usda.gov/) divides North America, and by extension the zone system used worldwide, into 13 zones based on average annual extreme minimum winter temperature. Each zone spans a 10°F (5.6°C) band. It's a shorthand for cold-hardiness limits only, not a full climate description, and says nothing about summer heat, rainfall, or soil.
