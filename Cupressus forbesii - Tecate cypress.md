Tecate cypress (_Cupressus forbesii_) is an evergreen tree native to the mountains overlooking Tijuana, Mexico, staying uniformly tight and symmetrical with green-gray foliage and cherry-red bark.[^1] It grows 3-10 m tall and 3-5 m wide.[^1]
