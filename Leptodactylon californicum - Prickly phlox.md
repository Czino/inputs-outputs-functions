Prickly phlox (_Leptodactylon californicum_, syn. _Linanthus californicus_) is a one- to three-foot subshrub of Southern California chaparral and oak woodland, with very fragrant bright pink spring flowers, needing excellent drainage or it rots.[^1] It grows 0.3-1 m tall and wide.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
