Desert almond (_Prunus fasciculata_) is a deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] desert washes and dry slopes across the [[North America|North American]] Southwest, its fruit considered a delicacy by several native peoples. As with other _Prunus_ species, the seed contains cyanogenic compounds and should not be eaten if it tastes bitter.
