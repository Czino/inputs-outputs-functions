Wild rye (_Secale sylvestre_) is a drought-tolerant perennial cereal grass native to [[Europe]] and temperate Asia, its seed cooked whole or ground into flour.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Drought-tolerance]][^1]
