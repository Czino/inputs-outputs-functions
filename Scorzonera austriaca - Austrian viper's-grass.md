Austrian viper's-grass (_Scorzonera austriaca_) is a perennial herb native to [[Europe]] to east [[Asia]], growing in dry places from central France to Czechoslovakia, south to S. Italy and Bulgaria.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]
