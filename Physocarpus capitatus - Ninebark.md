Ninebark (_Physocarpus capitatus_) is a deciduous shrub of the California coast and Sierra Nevada, growing about 5 feet high and wide with leaves that turn red to orange in fall, tolerant of seasonal flooding and serpentine soil.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and 0.5-2 m wide.[^1]
