Three-cornered leek (_Allium triquetrum_) is a bulbous perennial with a mild garlic flavour, used as a leek substitute — bulb, leaves, and flowers are all edible. It spreads readily and is considered invasive outside its native Mediterranean range, including in parts of the UK, [[North America]], and [[Australia]].
