Monkeyflower savory (_Satureja mimuloides_) is a rhizome-spreading perennial of California chaparral and Douglas fir forest, growing in wet spots in full sun, with fragrant deep red-orange flowers that smell of mild spearmint.[^1] It grows 1 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
