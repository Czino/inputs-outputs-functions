Coast buckwheat (_Eriogonum parvifolium_) is an evergreen perennial of California's coastal bluffs and dunes, a host plant for many butterflies including the rare El Segundo blue. It grows 0.2-0.5 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
