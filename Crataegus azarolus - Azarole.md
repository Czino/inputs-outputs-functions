Azarole (_Crataegus azarolus_) is a deciduous tree or shrub known for its edible, apple-like fruits. The fruits are used fresh, in preserves, or for making liqueurs. Azarole is also appreciated for its ornamental flowers and foliage, as well as its medicinal properties.
