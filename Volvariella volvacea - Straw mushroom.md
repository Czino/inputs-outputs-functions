Straw mushroom (_Volvariella volvacea_) is a fast-fruiting tropical mushroom grown across Southeast Asia on paddy (rice) straw, often as a sideline to rice production.[^1]

## Inputs
- Substrate: paddy (rice) straw is the standard and highest-yielding choice; cotton waste, dried banana leaves, and oil palm bunch waste are also used, though yields run lower than with paddy straw.[^1]
- Simple method: thoroughly moistened paddy straw left in a shaded spot (e.g. under trees), no building or equipment required.[^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]]: a market mushroom crop integrated into rice-growing smallholdings across Viet Nam, Indonesia, and Malaysia.[^1]

## Type
- [[Edible fungi]]
