Common trumpet creeper (_Campsis radicans_) is a vigorous, self-clinging deciduous climbing vine native to [[North America|southeastern North America]].[^1] It is grown for its showy orange-red trumpet flowers and as a sunny ground cover, but can become invasive outside its native range.[^1] It climbs up to 12 m,[^1] with a spread of 2.5-4 m.[^2]
