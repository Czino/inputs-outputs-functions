Slender monkey flower (_Diplacus grandiflorus_, formerly _Mimulus grandiflorus_) is a showy California native sub-shrub of granitic soils, visited by hummingbirds, that goes semi-deciduous if left dry through summer.[^1] It grows 0.3-0.7 m tall and 0.3-1 m wide.[^1]
