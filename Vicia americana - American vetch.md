American vetch (_Vicia americana_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial legume native to [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairie and open woodland, its young shoots and seeds eaten and its leaves used in traditional medicine.
