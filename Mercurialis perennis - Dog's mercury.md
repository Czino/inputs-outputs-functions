Dog's mercury (_Mercurialis perennis_) is a fast-spreading perennial herb native to [[Europe|Europe]] and southwest [[Asia|Asia]], common in woodland shade. All parts are poisonous fresh, though drying or heat destroys the toxin.[^1]
