Tree wormwood (_Artemisia arborescens_) is a fast-growing evergreen [[Mediterranean]] shrub with silvery, aromatic foliage, grown as a xeriscape and border plant. It has no known edible use, and skin contact can cause dermatitis in sensitive people.
