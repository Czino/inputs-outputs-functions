Northern California black walnut (_Juglans hindsii_) is a deciduous tree native to California, in western [[North America]], its thick-shelled seed edible and pressed for a short-lived oil, and historically used as rootstock for grafted English walnut.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
