Santa Barbara ceanothus (_Ceanothus impressus_) is a nitrogen-fixing evergreen shrub native to California's coastal chaparral, in western [[North America]], grown for its blue flower clusters and a green dye extracted from them.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Ceanothus spp. - Wild lilac]]
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
