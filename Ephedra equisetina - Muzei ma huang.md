Muzei ma huang (_Ephedra equisetina_) is an evergreen, jointed-stem shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] rocky steppe across [[Asia|Central Asia]], its alkaloid-rich stems a traditional source of medicinal ephedrine and its fruit edible.

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs

- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
