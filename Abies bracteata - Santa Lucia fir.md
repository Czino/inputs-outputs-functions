Santa Lucia fir (_Abies bracteata_) is a slow-growing evergreen conifer endemic to the Santa Lucia Mountains of coastal California, valued as a tight, symmetrical specimen tree.[^1] It grows 5-20 m tall and 1-10 m wide.[^1]
