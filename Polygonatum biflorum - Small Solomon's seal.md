Small Solomon's seal (_Polygonatum biflorum_) is a shade-tolerant woodland perennial of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] uplands, its young shoots and root eaten as a vegetable and its rhizome used medicinally (the berries, unlike the root, are considered possibly poisonous).
