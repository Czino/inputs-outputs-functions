Shrubby everlasting (_Helichrysum stoechas_) is a perennial herb of dry, sandy soils across southern and western [[Europe]], closely related to the more commonly grown [[Helichrysum italicum - Curry plant|curry plant]]. Its flowers and stem tops were once used to treat colds, though this medicinal use is now considered obsolete.
