Mediterranean saltbush (Atriplex halimus) is an evergreen shrub tolerant of saline, alkaline, and coastal conditions. Its leaves are edible with a salty flavour, and it is widely planted as a hedge, windbreak, and livestock forage on poor or saline ground.
