A contour bund is a linear embankment of earth, rock, or woody material built along a slope's contour lines to slow runoff, increase infiltration, and stabilize soil.[^1] Bunds require substantial upfront labor to build and ongoing maintenance to stay effective.[^1] In dryland farming they are often paired with [[Zai pit|zai pits]] to concentrate harvested water and fertility at the planting point.[^2]
