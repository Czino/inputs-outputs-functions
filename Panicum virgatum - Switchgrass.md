Switchgrass (_Panicum virgatum_) is a tall perennial bunchgrass native to the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and tallgrass prairies of [[North America]], grown for forage, hay, and soil stabilization.

- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 12]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
