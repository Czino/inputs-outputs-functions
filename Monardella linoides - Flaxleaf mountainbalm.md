Flaxleaf mountainbalm (_Monardella linoides_ var. _stricta_) is a mounding, lemon-scented perennial of gravelly mountain soils in California, shaded by Jeffrey pine, Ceanothus, and Amelanchier, with pink flowers that attract many butterflies.[^1] It grows 0.1-0.2 m tall and 0.2-1 m wide.[^1]
