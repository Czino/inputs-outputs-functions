King's spear (_Asphodeline lutea_) is a perennial herb of dry grassland and rocky, often limestone, ground across [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] southern and eastern [[Europe]], North Africa, and east to the Caucasus. Its root was roasted and eaten like a potato by the ancient Greeks, and its young shoots and sweetly scented flowers are also eaten.[^1]
