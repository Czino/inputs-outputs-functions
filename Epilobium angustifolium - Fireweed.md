Fireweed (_Epilobium angustifolium_) is a tall, rhizomatous perennial herb native to [[Europe|Europe]], temperate [[Asia|Asia]] and [[North America|North America]], forming large colonizing patches on rocky ground, waste areas and woodland edges, especially after fire or other disturbance.[^1]
