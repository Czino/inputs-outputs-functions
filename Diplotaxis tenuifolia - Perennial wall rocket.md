Perennial wall rocket (_Diplotaxis tenuifolia_) is the plant commonly called "wild rocket," a [[Mediterranean]] perennial herb distinct from cultivated rocket (_Eruca vesicaria_). Its strongly cress-flavored leaves are picked continuously from spring through autumn.
