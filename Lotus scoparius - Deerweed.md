Deerweed (_Lotus scoparius_, syn. _Acmispon glaber_) is a short-lived, very drought-tolerant perennial in the pea family, a pioneer species of California chaparral and coastal sage scrub that fixes nitrogen and fades out as the plant community matures. It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 0.6-1 m wide.[^1]
