Golden yarrow (_Eriophyllum confertiflorum_) is a dryland perennial of California chaparral, coastal sage scrub, and oak woodland, 1 to 2 feet tall with bright yellow flowers from spring into early summer, valued as a butterfly plant.[^1] It grows 0.1-1 m tall and 0.3-0.5 m wide.[^1]
