Yellow butterfly weed (_Solidago confinis_) is a deciduous California perennial of seeps and moist clay soils from the coast to Death Valley, with two-foot plumes of yellow flowers suited to cut-flower use. It grows 0.7-1 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
