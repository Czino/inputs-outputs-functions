Yosemite sedge (_Carex sartwelliana_) is a medium-sized meadow sedge of the California mountains, forming a lumpy, lawn-like mat where it is happy.[^1] It grows 0.3-0.6 m tall and 0.1-0.3 m wide.[^1]
