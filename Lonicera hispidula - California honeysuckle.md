California honeysuckle (_Lonicera hispidula_) is a deciduous climbing shrub native to California and Washington, crawling up neighboring plants and bearing fragrant pink flowers and red berries that are edible but very sour and bitter. It grows 1-6 m tall and 0.1-0.3 m wide.[^1]
