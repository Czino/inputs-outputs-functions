Thyme-leaved savory (_Satureja thymbra_) is a shrub native to south-eastern [[Europe]] (the Balkans, Crete, Greece), found in sunny positions on dry rocky hills. Its peppery, thyme-like leaves are used as a condiment and tea, and a strong infusion of the herb is traditionally used to clean wine barrels before the new vintage.[^1]
