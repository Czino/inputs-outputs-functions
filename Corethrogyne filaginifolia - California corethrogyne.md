California corethrogyne (_Corethrogyne filaginifolia_) is a tough, semi-deciduous perennial of California's dry slopes, its violet, yellow-centered flowers turning scraggly looking in summer drought.[^1] It grows 0.2-1 m tall and 0.2-0.5 m wide.[^1]
