California barberry (_Mahonia pinnata_) is an upright evergreen shrub native to California, in western [[North America]], its tart fruit edible and its roots and leaves a source of green, violet, and blue-purple dyes.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Flammable]][^2]
