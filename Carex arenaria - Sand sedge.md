Sand sedge (_Carex arenaria_) is a clump-forming perennial sedge native to coastal [[Europe]], from Portugal and Spain to Norway and Finland, spreading by a vigorous creeping rootstock to form colonies on sandy sea shores, fixed dunes and wind-blown sand.
