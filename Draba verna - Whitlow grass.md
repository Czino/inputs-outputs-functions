Whitlow grass (_Draba verna_) is a small annual herb of most of [[Europe]], south to N. [[Africa]] and east to temperate [[Asia]]. Its leaves are eaten raw, and it has a folk-medicinal use as a treatment for whitlows.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
