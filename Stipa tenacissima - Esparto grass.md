Esparto grass (_Stipa tenacissima_) is a tough, wind-pollinated perennial bunchgrass of the dry, gypsum and calcareous soils of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] interior [[Europe|Spain]] and North Africa, long harvested for its leaf fibre.

- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs

- [[Wax]][^1]
