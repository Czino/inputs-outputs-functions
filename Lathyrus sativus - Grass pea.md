Grass pea (_Lathyrus sativus_) is an annual legume grown for its edible seed and pods and as a nitrogen-fixing green manure. The mature seed must be soaked and well cooked and kept to a small share of the diet, since eating too much over time can cause a nerve disorder called lathyrism.
