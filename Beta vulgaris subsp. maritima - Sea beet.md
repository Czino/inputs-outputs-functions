Sea beet (_Beta vulgaris_ subsp. _maritima_) is the wild ancestor of beet and chard, a coastal herb of [[Mediterranean]] and Atlantic shorelines. Its young leaves are eaten as a spinach substitute, becoming bitter as they mature.
