Rocky Mountain beeplant (_Cleome serrulata_) is an annual of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] plains and disturbed ground, historically processed into dried cakes as a staple food and valued as a major bee forage plant.
