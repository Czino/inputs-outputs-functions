Ground ivy (_Glechoma hederacea_) is a low, mat-forming evergreen perennial herb native to [[Europe|Europe]] and temperate [[Asia|Asia]], found on damp waste ground, hedgerows and woodland margins.[^1] It should be used with some caution medicinally — reports note possible toxicity to horses, and advise against its internal use during pregnancy or in cases of epilepsy or kidney disease.[^1]
