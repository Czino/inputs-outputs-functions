Prickly juniper (Juniperus oxycedrus) is an evergreen [[Mediterranean]] tree. Its wood yields cade oil through destructive distillation, a traditional antiseptic used to treat skin conditions like psoriasis and eczema. It can reach 10-15 m tall,[^4] though the RHS lists 2.5-4 m tall and wide in cultivation.[^5]
