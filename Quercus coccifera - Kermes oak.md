Kermes oak (Quercus coccifera) is a small, spiny-leaved evergreen shrub oak common in [[Mediterranean]] maquis. Its acorns are edible once leached of their bitter tannins. It usually stays under 2 m tall, occasionally becoming a small tree of 4-6 m,[^3] though the RHS lists 4-8 m tall and wide in cultivation.[^4]
