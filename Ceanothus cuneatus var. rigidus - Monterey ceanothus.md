Monterey ceanothus (_Ceanothus cuneatus_ var. _rigidus_, syn. _Ceanothus rigidus_) is a densely branched evergreen shrub native to California's Monterey Peninsula, growing 1.8-3 m tall and 2.4-3.6 m wide.[^2] Its seeds are edible, and its leaves and flowers can be steeped for tea.[^1]
