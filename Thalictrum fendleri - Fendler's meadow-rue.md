Fendler's meadow-rue (_Thalictrum fendleri_) is a perennial herb native to western [[North America]], its crushed leaves used as a wash for headaches and its whole plant yielding a yellow dye.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
