Greasewood (_Sarcobatus vermiculatus_) is a spiny deciduous shrub of alkaline and saline [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] flats and plains across western [[North America]], its young shoots and seeds eaten as greens and famine food.
