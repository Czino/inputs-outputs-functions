Western sweetshrub (_Calycanthus occidentalis_) is a deciduous shrub native to California stream banks, in western [[North America]], its dried aromatic bark used as a cinnamon and allspice substitute — though the whole plant contains the toxic alkaloid calycanthine and only the dried bark is used as a spice.[^1]
