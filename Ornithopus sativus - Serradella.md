Serradella (_Ornithopus sativus_) is an annual or short-lived perennial herb grown as a nitrogen-fixing green manure and weed-excluding ground cover.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Annual]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions

- [[Ground cover]][^1]
