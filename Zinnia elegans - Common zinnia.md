Common zinnia (_Zinnia elegans_) is an erect, fast-growing annual native to [[Mexico]], grown for its large, showy summer and fall flower heads in gold, orange, pink, purple, red and white.[^1] It grows up to 0.9 m tall and 0.3 m wide.[^1]
