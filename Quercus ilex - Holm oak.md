Holm oak (Quercus ilex) is an evergreen [[Mediterranean]] oak valued for its acorns and dense, durable wood. The acorns are edible once the bitter tannins are leached out, and the roasted seed is used as a [[Coffee substitute]]. It reaches up to 25 m tall, exceptionally 30 m,[^3] and over 8 m wide.[^4]
