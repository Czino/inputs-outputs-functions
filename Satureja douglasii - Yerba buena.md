Yerba buena (_Satureja douglasii_, now also classified as _Clinopodium douglasii_) is a creeping, flat evergreen perennial of shaded coastal woods from Los Angeles to British Columbia, its leaves used for a refreshing tea. It grows 0.1-0.2 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
