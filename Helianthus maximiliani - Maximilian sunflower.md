Maximilian sunflower (_Helianthus maximiliani_) is a tall, colony-forming perennial of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairies, grown for its edible tubers and oil-rich seed, similar to the Jerusalem artichoke.
