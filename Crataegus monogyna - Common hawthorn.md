Common hawthorn (_Crataegus monogyna_) is a thorny deciduous shrub native across [[Europe|Europe]], valued for its edible fruit and flowers and its long history as a heart-health medicinal. It tolerates drought once established and is a diagnostic shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe scrub in Central and Eastern Europe.[^1]
