Coastal goldenbush (_Isocoma veneta_ var. _vernoniodes_, syn. _Haplopappus venetus_) is a stress-deciduous perennial that grows right along California's coastal bluffs, sometimes hanging over the edge. It grows 0.1-0.5 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
