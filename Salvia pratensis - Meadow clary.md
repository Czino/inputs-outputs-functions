Meadow clary (_Salvia pratensis_) is a cold-hardy perennial herb of old chalk and limestone grasslands across [[Europe]], from Britain east to the Crimea, and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] grassland.[^2]
