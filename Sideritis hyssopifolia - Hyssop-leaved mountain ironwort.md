Hyssop-leaved mountain ironwort (_Sideritis hyssopifolia_) is an evergreen shrub of limestone mountains in interior [[Europe|Spain]] and neighbouring France, Switzerland, and Italy. Its aromatic leaves are dried for an herbal tea traditionally drunk in the Spanish Pyrenees and used to flavour herbal liqueurs, and it has a long history of use for digestive complaints.
