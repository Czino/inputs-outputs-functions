Mediterranean hartwort (_Tordylium apulum_) is a perennial herb of cultivated and waste ground across [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]] to western Asia. Its young leaves are eaten as a vegetable in Greece and used as a condiment in Italy.[^1]
