California flannelbush (_Fremontodendron californicum_) is an evergreen shrub native to [[North America|California]], its bark used for cordage and as a poultice.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

- [[Fibre]][^1]
- [[Wood]][^1]
