California mugwort (_Artemisia douglasiana_) is an aromatic perennial herb native to stream banks, ditches, and other disturbed, moist ground in California, its leaves rubbed on skin as a traditional, unverified folk remedy for poison oak rash.[^1] It grows 0.7-1 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
