Purple moor-grass (_Molinia caerulea_) is a deciduous, clump-forming perennial grass of moist heathland, bogs, and moorland across [[Europe]], west Asia, and north Africa.[^1] Its dense tussocks and deep roots make it resistant to heath fires.[^2]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Type

- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
