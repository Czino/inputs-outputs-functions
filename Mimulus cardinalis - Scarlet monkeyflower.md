Scarlet monkeyflower (_Mimulus cardinalis_, syn. _Erythranthe cardinalis_) is an evergreen perennial of California mountain streambanks and riparian areas, an aggressive seeder grown for revegetation and for its red, hummingbird-pollinated flowers. It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 0.3-1 m wide.[^1]
