Bush morning glory (_Ipomoea leptophylla_) is a deep-rooted perennial herb of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] plains and dry sandy banks across the southern [[North America|Great Plains]], its large storage root a traditional food of Plains peoples and used medicinally.
