Parish's spike rush (_Eleocharis parishii_, also spelled _Heleocharis parishii_) is a creeping perennial rush of seasonally wet sites across the southwestern United States, dying back into the ground each winter and reappearing in spring. It grows 0.1-0.2 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
