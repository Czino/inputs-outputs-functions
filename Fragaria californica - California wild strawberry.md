California wild strawberry (_Fragaria californica_) is a perennial herb native to [[North America|California]], spreading by runners as a ground cover, its aromatic fruit edible raw.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Fragaria spp. - Strawberry]]
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Ground cover]][^1]
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
