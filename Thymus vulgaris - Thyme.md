Thyme (Thymus vulgaris) is an evergreen herb used in cooking for its aromatic leaves. It also has medicinal properties and is used as a ground cover in gardens, attracting pollinators and repelling pests like cabbage worms and whiteflies. It grows up to 30 cm tall and 40 cm wide.[^3]
