Green monardella (_Monardella viridis_) is an evergreen perennial of dry slopes and ridges in Sonoma County, California, with fragrant foliage and purple to violet flowers in early summer.[^1] It grows 0.3-0.5 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
