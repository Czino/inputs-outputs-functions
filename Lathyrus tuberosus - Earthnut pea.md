Earthnut pea (_Lathyrus tuberosus_) is a perennial legume of [[Europe]] and western [[Asia]] grown for its sweet, starchy edible root, though yields are low. Like other _Lathyrus_, its seed can contain a toxic amino acid that causes lathyrism if eaten in large amounts.
