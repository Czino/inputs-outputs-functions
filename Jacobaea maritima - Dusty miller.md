Dusty miller (_Jacobaea maritima_) is an evergreen shrub with silvery, felted foliage, native to [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] coasts and naturalized on maritime cliffs elsewhere, including southwest England.[^1] Its leaf juice has an ophthalmic medicinal use, but the plant contains liver-toxic pyrrolizidine alkaloids and should not be used internally.[^1]
