Skunkbush sumac (_Rhus trilobata_) is a deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] plains and foothills, its tart fruit used fresh, dried, or as a lemonade-like drink.

- [[Hardiness zone 4]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 5]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 6]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

- [[Attracting bees]][^1]
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
