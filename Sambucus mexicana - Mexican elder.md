Mexican elder (_Sambucus mexicana_) is a deciduous shrub native to [[North America|the southwestern United States and Mexico]], closely related to [[Sambucus caerulea - Blue elderberry|blue elderberry]], its flowers and fruit edible once dried or cooked.[^1]
