Small tumbleweed mustard (_Sisymbrium loeselii_) is an annual herb native to southeastern Europe and temperate Asia, now naturalized as a weed of disturbed ground, roadsides, prairies, and railroad tracks across much of Europe, Asia, and North America.[^1]
