California goldenrod (_Solidago californica_, syn. _Solidago velutina_ subsp. _californica_) is a deciduous California perennial with showy yellow flowers in late summer and fall, growing in seasonally wet ground that dries by summer. It grows 0.4-1 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
