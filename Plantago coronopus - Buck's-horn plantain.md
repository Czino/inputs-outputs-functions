Buck's-horn plantain (_Plantago coronopus_) is an annual or perennial herb of coastal [[Europe]], North Africa and western [[Asia]], growing in sandy and rocky ground. Its tender young leaves, one of the ingredients of the Italian "misticanze" salad mix, are among the better-tasting of the plantains.[^1]
