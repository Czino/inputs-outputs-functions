Mat amaranth (_Amaranthus blitoides_) is a low, mat-forming annual herb native to [[North America]], now naturalized across [[Europe]] and elsewhere. It grows about 0.2 m tall and 0.3 m wide,[^1] and its seeds and leaves are edible, the seeds toasted, boiled, or ground into flour, the leaves used as a [[Spinach]] substitute once boiled.[^1]
