Beach evening primrose (_Camissonia cheiranthifolia_) is a perennial of coastal sand dunes from Santa Barbara north into Oregon, with limited use beyond stabilizing dunes or serving as a temporary cover in beach sand.[^1] It grows 0.1-0.6 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
