Bilberry (_Vaccinium myrtillus_) is a low, deciduous shrub native to [[Europe]], from Iceland south and east to Spain and the Caucasus, grown for its small, sweet, dark blue edible berries. It is distinct from the cultivated highbush blueberry (see [[Vaccinium spp. - Blueberry]]).
