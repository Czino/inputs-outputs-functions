Stinging nettle (_Urtica dioica_) is a fast-growing perennial herb known for its stinging hairs, grown as a nutrient-rich potherb and used extensively in traditional medicine. It is a larger, perennial relative of the vault's [[Urtica urens - Annual nettle|annual nettle]].
