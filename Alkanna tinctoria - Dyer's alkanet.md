Dyer's alkanet (_Alkanna tinctoria_) is a low-growing perennial herb of the [[Mediterranean]] basin, grown for the red dye its roots yield.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]
