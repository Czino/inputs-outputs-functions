Hoary plantain (_Plantago media_) is a perennial herb of dry and calcareous grassland across southern [[Europe]] to temperate [[Asia]], and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] Its young leaves are eaten in salads and its sweetly scented inflorescence is sucked by children.[^1]
