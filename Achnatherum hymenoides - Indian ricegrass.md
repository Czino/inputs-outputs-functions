Indian ricegrass (_Achnatherum hymenoides_) is a drought-tolerant bunchgrass native to sandy, [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] plains and deserts of [[North America]], historically an important staple grain for Native peoples of the Great Basin and Southwest.
