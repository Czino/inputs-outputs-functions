Red sandspurry (_Spergularia rubra_) is an annual/biennial herb native to [[Europe]], south and east from Norway to N. Africa and Asia, found on open gravelly or sandy habitats, dunes and heaths.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Annual]]
- [[Biennial]]
