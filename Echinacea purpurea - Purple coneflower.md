Purple coneflower (_Echinacea purpurea_) is a cold-hardy perennial herb of dry open woods, [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] prairies, and barrens in eastern [[North America]], with edible leaves and extensive medicinal use.
