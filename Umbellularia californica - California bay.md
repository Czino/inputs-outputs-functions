California bay (Umbellularia californica) is an evergreen tree native to the Pacific coast of [[North America]], distinct from the Mediterranean [[Laurus nobilis - Bay laurel|bay laurel]] despite sharing the common name "bay laurel." Its aromatic leaves are used as a bay-leaf substitute, and its seeds can be roasted and eaten or ground into flour.[^1]
