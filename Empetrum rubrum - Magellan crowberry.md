Magellan crowberry (_Empetrum rubrum_) is an evergreen groundcover shrub of heath and open ground across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] Patagonia and Tierra del Fuego in [[South America]], its fruit edible and tonic.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type

- [[Evergreen]]
