California fuchsia (_Epilobium canum_) is a California native perennial grown for its red, orange, or pink hummingbird-pollinated flowers in late summer and fall. It was formerly split across several species in the genus _Zauschneria_ (including _Z. californica_, _Z. cana_, _Z. latifolia_, and _Z. septentrionalis_), now treated as synonyms or subspecies of this one species.[^1]
