Bent grass (_Agrostis pallens_) is a low, creeping perennial grass of California meadows and woodlands, delicate and showy mainly in masses, sometimes used as a spring lawn grass.[^1] It grows 0.2-0.8 m tall and 0.2-0.6 m wide.[^1]
