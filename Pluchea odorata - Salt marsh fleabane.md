Salt marsh fleabane (_Pluchea odorata_) is a strongly scented, almost succulent perennial of coastal salt marsh and freshwater marsh in California, its yarrow-like flower clusters drawing bees, flies and wasps. It grows 0.5-1 m tall and wide.[^1]
