Scotch broom (_Cytisus scoparius_) is a fast-growing, nitrogen-fixing, deciduous shrub native to [[Europe|Europe]]. Its flower buds are pickled as a caper substitute and its bark yields fibre and dye, but it is considered invasive in parts of North America and New Zealand.[^1]
