Hairy-leaf ceanothus (_Ceanothus oliganthus_) is a sprawling evergreen shrub of north-facing chaparral slopes in coastal Southern California, valued as a bird and wildlife plant and sometimes grown as a hedge.[^1] It grows 2-4 m tall and wide.[^1]
