Fiddleneck (_Phacelia tanacetifolia_) is an annual herb native to [[North America]] (Arizona, California, southern Nevada, and northern Baja California), often naturalized elsewhere. It grows about 0.6 m tall and 0.3 m wide,[^1] and is grown as a bee-forage and green-manure crop.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Annual]]

## Functions
- [[Attracting bees]][^1]
- [[Green manure]][^1]
