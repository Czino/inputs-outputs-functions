Opium poppy (_Papaver somniferum_) is an annual herb grown for its edible seeds and, in the dried latex, a source of narcotic and medicinal compounds; cultivation of the plant for latex is legally restricted in many countries.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
