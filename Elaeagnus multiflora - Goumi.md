Goumi is a deciduous shrub known for its small, tart, and sweet red berries. The berries are rich in vitamins and antioxidants, used fresh or in jams and desserts. Goumi is also valued for its nitrogen-fixing ability, improving soil fertility and serving as a hardy, resilient plant in various landscapes.
