Summer holly (_Comarostaphylis diversifolia_) is a slow-growing evergreen shrub native to the chaparral of coastal San Diego County and Baja California, needing good drainage and little summer water once established.[^1] It grows 1-6 m tall and 1-5 m wide.[^1]
