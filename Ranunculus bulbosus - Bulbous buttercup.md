Bulbous buttercup (_Ranunculus bulbosus_) is a perennial herb of meadows, dry pastures and grassy slopes across [[Europe]], south and east to N. [[Africa]] and Greece. All parts of the plant are acrid and poisonous when fresh, and it has a folk-medicinal use as a rubefacient and painkiller.[^1]
