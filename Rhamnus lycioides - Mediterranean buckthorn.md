Mediterranean buckthorn (Rhamnus lycioides) is a deciduous shrub or small tree known for its thorny branches and small, black fruits. It is commonly used in hedgerows and as a natural barrier due to its dense and spiky growth. Mediterranean buckthorn can also provide wildlife habitat and support biodiversity in garden settings.
