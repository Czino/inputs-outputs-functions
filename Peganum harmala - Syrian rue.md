Syrian rue (_Peganum harmala_) is a perennial herb of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe across [[Europe|the Mediterranean and south-eastern Europe]], its seed used as a spice and dye source and in traditional medicine; the seed also carries hallucinogenic and narcotic alkaloids and should be used with caution.
