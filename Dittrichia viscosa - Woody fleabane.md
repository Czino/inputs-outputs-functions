Woody fleabane (_Dittrichia viscosa_), also called false yellowhead, is a sticky, strong-smelling perennial herb common throughout the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] Basin, growing on dry riverbeds, abandoned ground and disturbed roadsides.[^2] A yellow dye is obtained from its roots.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]][^1]

## Functions
- [[Attracting beneficial insects]][^2]
