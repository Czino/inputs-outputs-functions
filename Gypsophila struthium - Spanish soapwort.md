Spanish soapwort (_Gypsophila struthium_) is a low, mat-forming perennial herb of gypsum and alkaline soils in southern [[Europe]], including interior [[Europe|Spain]]. Its Spanish name, _jabonera_, reflects a traditional use of the saponin-rich root as a soap substitute; the root itself is used as an external wash for skin diseases.
