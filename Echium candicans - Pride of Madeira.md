Pride of Madeira (_Echium candicans_) is a perennial subshrub native to Madeira, grown for its large spikes of white to blue flowers.[^1] Its hairy foliage is a skin irritant, so it's best handled with gloves.[^1] It grows 1.5-2.5 m tall and wide.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Attracting bees]][^1]
