Prickly scorpion's-tail (_Scorpiurus muricatus_) is an annual, nitrogen-fixing legume herb of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]]. Its young seedpods, shaped like small caterpillars, are eaten raw or cooked.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Annual]]

## Functions
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
