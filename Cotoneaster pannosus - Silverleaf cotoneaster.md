Silverleaf cotoneaster (_Cotoneaster pannosus_) is a shrub native to south-western China, named for the white, woolly undersides of its leaves.[^1] Its small red berries are eaten and spread by birds.[^2] It grows 1-3.5 m tall.[^1]
