Kurrajong (_Brachychiton populneus_) is a fast-growing evergreen tree native to coastal and sub-coastal [[Australia]], often on limestone.[^1] Its seeds are eaten raw or roasted, roasted seed is used as a [[Coffee substitute]], and its young roots are eaten like yam.[^1] It grows up to 18 m tall and 18 m wide.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Coffee substitute]][^1]
- [[Fibre]][^1]

- [[Evergreen]]
