A grassed waterway is a shaped, broad, shallow channel established with vegetation to carry concentrated runoff from terraces, diversions, or gullies at a non-erosive speed to a stable outlet.[^1] It prevents gully formation and protects water quality by filtering sediment as water passes through the vegetation.[^1]
