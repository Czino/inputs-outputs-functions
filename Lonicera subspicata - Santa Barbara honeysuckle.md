Santa Barbara honeysuckle (_Lonicera subspicata_ var. _subspicata_) is an evergreen perennial of California chaparral around Santa Barbara, growing 2 to 5 feet with creamy yellow flower clusters.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and 1 m wide.[^1]
