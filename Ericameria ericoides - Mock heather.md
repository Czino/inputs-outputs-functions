Mock heather (_Ericameria ericoides_) is an evergreen shrub of California coastal dunes, covered each year in bright yellow flowers and tolerant of pure beach sand and salt spray.[^1] It grows 1-1.1 m tall and about 1 m wide.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
