Bush anemone (_Carpenteria californica_) is an evergreen shrub endemic to the foothills of Fresno County, California, grown for its fragrant white, anemone-like flowers blooming in June and July.[^1] It grows 2-3 m tall and 1-4 m wide.[^1]
