Prickly rose (_Rosa acicularis_) is a cold-hardy deciduous shrub native to [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and boreal [[North America]] and [[Asia]], its vitamin-C-rich hips widely eaten and used medicinally.
