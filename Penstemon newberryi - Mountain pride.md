Mountain pride (_Penstemon newberryi_) is a perennial of granite rock and scree at 5,000-9,000 ft in the Sierra Nevada, with pink flowers, growing where it is snow-covered all winter. It grows 0.1-0.5 m tall and 0.2-1 m wide.[^1]
