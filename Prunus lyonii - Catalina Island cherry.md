Catalina Island cherry (_Prunus lyonii_) is an evergreen tree native to the Channel Islands of California, in [[North America]], closely related to [[Prunus ilicifolia - Hollyleaf cherry|hollyleaf cherry]], with fruit edible raw and a seed edible once leached of bitterness.[^1]
