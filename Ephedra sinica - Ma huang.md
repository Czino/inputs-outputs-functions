Ma huang (_Ephedra sinica_) is a low evergreen shrub of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe of Mongolia and northern China, its fruit edible and its stems the source of ephedrine, a potent and tightly regulated stimulant compound long used in Chinese medicine.
