Mock privet (Phillyrea latifolia) is an evergreen [[Mediterranean]] tree in the olive family, grown as a clipped hedge and for its fine-grained wood, which produces an excellent charcoal.

- [[Hardiness zone 6]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Wood]][^1]
- [[Charcoal]][^1]

## Type
- [[Tree]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Hedge]][^1]
- [[Flammable]][^2][^3]
