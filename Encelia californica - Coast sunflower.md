Coast sunflower (_Encelia californica_) is an evergreen perennial shrub of California chaparral, coastal sage scrub, and coastal strand, with daisy-like yellow flowers prized for cutting.[^1] It grows 0.5-1.5 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
