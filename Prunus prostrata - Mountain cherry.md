Mountain cherry (_Prunus prostrata_) is a low, spreading deciduous shrub of rocky mountains from southeastern Europe to western Asia, 0.6-1 m tall and considerably wider than it is tall.[^1][^2] Its fruit and seed can be eaten raw or cooked, but are not very palatable, and bitter seeds should be avoided since they contain cyanide-releasing compounds.[^1]
