Spiny broom (_Calicotome spinosa_) is a deciduous shrub native to the western [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] region of [[Europe]] (Spain, France, Italy) and [[North Africa]], found on dry stony ground and evergreen scrub near the coast, especially after woodland clearance.[^1]
