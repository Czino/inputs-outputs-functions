Santa Susana monkeyflower (_Diplacus rutilus_) is a perennial with large velvet-red flowers, growing in rock outcroppings of decomposed granite north of Los Angeles.[^1] It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 0.2-1 m wide.[^1]
