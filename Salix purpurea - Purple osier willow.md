Purple osier willow (_Salix purpurea_) is a fast-growing deciduous tree of riverbanks and wet ground across [[Europe]], [[North Africa]], and [[Asia]]; its bitter inner bark is a famine food eaten only when all else fails, not a regular one.[^1]
