Judas tree (Cercis siliquastrum) is a deciduous [[Mediterranean]] tree with pink flowers that appear directly on the bark before the leaves, and its flowers and young seedpods are edible. Despite being a legume, it does not fix nitrogen: it belongs to Cercidoideae, an early-diverging legume subfamily that lacks the rhizobial nodulation symbiosis.[^3]
