Blue grama (_Bouteloua gracilis_) is a low-growing perennial bunchgrass of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] short-grass prairie, its seed traditionally ground into flour or eaten as porridge.
