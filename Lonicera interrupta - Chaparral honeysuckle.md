Chaparral honeysuckle (_Lonicera interrupta_) is a sprawling, deciduous vining shrub of California chaparral and oak woodland, tolerant of full sun to shade, with an edible but bitter berry.[^1] It grows 0.7-2 m tall and 0.8-1 m wide.[^1]
