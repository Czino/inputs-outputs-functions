Hooker's evening primrose (_Oenothera elata_ subsp. _hookeri_, syn. _Oenothera hookeri_) is a biennial or perennial of dry open soils, meadows, and roadsides in northwestern North America, growing to about 0.8 m.[^1] Its roots are cooked like parsnip, and its young shoots and unripe seed pods are eaten as a potherb.[^1]
