Julian savory (_Micromeria juliana_) is a deciduous shrub of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]], found in rocky places and other dry open habitats. Its leaves have long been used as a diuretic for kidney and bladder ailments.[^1]
