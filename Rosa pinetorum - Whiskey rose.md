Whiskey rose (_Rosa pinetorum_) is a deciduous shrub rarely seen in the wild, found under live oaks and bays on cold north-facing slopes and at redwood-forest edges in California, with showy, fragrant flowers and edible, fragrant fruit. It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
