Canada milkvetch (_Astragalus canadensis_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial legume of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairie and open woodland. Its root is edible raw or boiled, though a bitter root can indicate toxic alkaloids and should be avoided.
