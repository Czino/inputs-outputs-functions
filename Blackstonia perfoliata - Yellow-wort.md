Yellow-wort (_Blackstonia perfoliata_) is an annual herb of calcareous grassland and dunes across [[Europe]], north Africa, and west Asia, grown for the yellow dye obtained from the plant.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Annual]][^1]
