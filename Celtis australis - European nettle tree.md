The European nettle tree (Celtis australis) is a deciduous tree native to the [[Mediterranean]] region, grown for its shade, drought tolerance, and small sweet fruit. It usually grows 15-20 m tall, exceptionally 25-30 m,[^2] and 12-21 m tall and 12-15 m wide in cultivation.[^3]
