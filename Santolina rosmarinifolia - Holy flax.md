Holy flax (_Santolina rosmarinifolia_) is a low evergreen [[Mediterranean]] shrub used as a formal hedge and firebreak plant. Its leaves and flowers add an olive-like flavor to savory dishes; bruised leaves can irritate sensitive skin.
