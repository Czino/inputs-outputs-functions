Large leaved lime (_Tilia platyphyllos_) is a long-lived deciduous tree native to woodland on base-rich soils across [[Europe]].[^1] Its young leaves are eaten raw, its flowers are brewed into a calming tea, and its spring sap can be drunk or boiled into syrup.[^1] It grows up to 30 m tall and 20 m wide.[^1]
