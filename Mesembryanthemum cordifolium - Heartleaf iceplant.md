Heartleaf iceplant (_Mesembryanthemum cordifolium_), also sold as baby sun rose, is an evergreen succulent groundcover native to [[Africa]]. It grows in a prostrate, mat-forming habit about 0.1 m tall and spreading 0.3-0.6 m wide, rooting at the nodes where its trailing stems touch the soil.[^1]
