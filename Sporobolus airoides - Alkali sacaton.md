Alkali sacaton (_Sporobolus airoides_) is a perennial bunchgrass of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] alkaline flats and rangeland across western [[North America]], its seed historically ground into flour as a famine food.

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]
