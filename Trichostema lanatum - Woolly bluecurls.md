Woolly bluecurls (_Trichostema lanatum_) is an aromatic evergreen shrub native to [[North America|California]]'s chaparral, its leaves and flowering tops brewed into a tea and used traditionally to ease stomach ailments.[^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Flammable]][^2]
