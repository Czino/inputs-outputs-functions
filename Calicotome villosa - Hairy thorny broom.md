Hairy thorny broom (_Calicotome villosa_) is a deciduous, spiny shrub of dry stony scrub near the coast across the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]], especially common in areas after woodland clearance. As a legume, it fixes nitrogen through root-nodule symbiosis with soil bacteria, and is used for low hedging.[^1]
