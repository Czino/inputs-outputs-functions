Foothill ash (_Fraxinus dipetala_) is a deciduous shrub or small tree of California chaparral and oak woodland, growing on slopes and near springs rather than in creek beds, with fragrant flower clusters in spring.[^1] It grows 3-7 m tall and 3-4 m wide.[^1]
