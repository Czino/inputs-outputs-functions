Early dog-violet (_Viola reichenbachiana_) is a perennial herb native to [[Europe]] and temperate [[Asia]], found in woods and hedgebanks, usually on calcareous soils.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
