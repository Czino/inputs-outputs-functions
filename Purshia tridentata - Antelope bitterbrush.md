Antelope bitterbrush (_Purshia tridentata_) is a gray, small-leaved, nitrogen-fixing shrub of dry slopes on the east side of the Sierra Nevada, an important winter browse plant whose seeds feed birds and chipmunks. It grows 1-2 m tall and 1-3 m wide.[^1]
