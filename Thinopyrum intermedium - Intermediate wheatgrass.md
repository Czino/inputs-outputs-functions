Intermediate wheatgrass (_Thinopyrum intermedium_) is a long-lived perennial grass of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppes of [[Europe]] and western [[Asia]], from Spain to central Asia. Its seed is eaten as a grain and the plant is widely used to stabilize disturbed soils; it can become weedy or dominate a site where it is well adapted.
