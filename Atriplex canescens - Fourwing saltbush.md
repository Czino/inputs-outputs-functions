Fourwing saltbush (_Atriplex canescens_) is an evergreen shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and saline rangeland across western [[North America]], valued for its edible leaves and seeds and its tolerance of poor, alkaline soils.
