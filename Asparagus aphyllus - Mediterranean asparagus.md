Mediterranean asparagus (_Asparagus aphyllus_) is a dioecious perennial of hedges and rocky limestone hills across [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]] and [[North Africa]]. Its young shoots are eaten raw or cooked like garden asparagus, with a slightly bitter flavour.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
