Algerian tea (_Paronychia argentea_) is an evergreen perennial herb of dry rocky habitats from southern [[Europe]] to southwest [[Asia]]. Its flowers are infused as a tea, and its leaves are used as an aphrodisiac and diuretic.[^1]
