Chinese pea shrub (_Caragana sinica_) is a nitrogen-fixing deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and cool-temperate [[Asia|China]], its edible flowers and medicinal root used in traditional Chinese and Korean herbalism.
