Marsh baccharis (_Baccharis douglasii_) is a fast-growing perennial shrub of wet and seasonally flooded ground from Baja California to Humboldt County, an important nectar source for predatory wasps and other beneficial insects.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and wide.[^1]
