Pheasant's eye (_Adonis vernalis_) is a perennial herb of central and southern [[Europe]] and a constant species of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] It contains cardiac glycosides used in European herbal medicine and should only be used under professional medical supervision.[^1]
