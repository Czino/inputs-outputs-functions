Purging flax (_Linum catharticum_) is a small annual herb of grassland, dunes and moors across [[Europe]], most commonly on calcareous soils. It has a long history of use as a gentle herbal laxative.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
