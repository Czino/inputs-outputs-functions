Boz-Pirnal oak (_Quercus aucheri_) is a small evergreen oak of Greece, Turkey, and the East Aegean Islands, growing to 5 m by 4 m in [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] phrygana scrub. Its acorns are edible roasted, and its astringent galls and bark are used medicinally and for dye.[^1]
