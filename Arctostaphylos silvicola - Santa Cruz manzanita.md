Santa Cruz manzanita (_Arctostaphylos silvicola_) is a gray-leaved evergreen shrub native to old sand dunes in the Santa Cruz Mountains of California, requiring well drained soil and intolerant of summer water once established.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and wide.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]

- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
