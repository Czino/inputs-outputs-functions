Small-flowered cistus (_Cistus parviflorus_) is an evergreen shrub of the eastern [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] (Italy, the Aegean), with a spreading habit and found wild in calcareous shrubland. It is grown ornamentally, including as a groundcover, and like other rockroses is very drought tolerant once established.[^1]

- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
