Basil thyme (_Clinopodium acinos_) is an annual-to-short-lived-perennial herb native to almost all of [[Europe]], from Scandinavia south to Spain and east to Turkey, found on dry sunny banks and chalky, gravelly or sandy fields, including [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe-over-limestone habitat.
