Sessile-leaved broom (_Cytisophyllum sessilifolium_) is a nitrogen-fixing deciduous shrub native to eastern Spain, southern France and Italy, growing in [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] scrub, dry oak forest and open pine woodland.[^1][^2][^3] It is the only species in its genus.[^2]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Deciduous]][^3]

## Functions
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
