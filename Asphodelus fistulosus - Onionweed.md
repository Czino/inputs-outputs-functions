Onionweed (_Asphodelus fistulosus_) is a short-lived perennial, often mistaken for an annual, of southern [[Europe]] and western [[Asia]] in the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] region. Its root is eaten cooked, but it has become a noxious weed in California and other Mediterranean-climate regions.[^1]
