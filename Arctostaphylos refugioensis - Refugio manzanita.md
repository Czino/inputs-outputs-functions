Refugio manzanita (_Arctostaphylos refugioensis_) is an evergreen shrub native to the chaparral of the Santa Ynez Mountains near Santa Barbara, California, thriving in full sun with little supplemental water.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and 1-2.5 m wide.[^1]
