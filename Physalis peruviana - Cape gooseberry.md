Cape gooseberry (_Physalis peruviana_) is a perennial herb native to [[South America]] (Venezuela, Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador and Peru) and widely naturalized elsewhere, grown for its sweet-tart orange berries, which are wrapped in a papery, toxic calyx that is discarded before eating.[^1] It grows up to 1.2 m tall.[^1]
