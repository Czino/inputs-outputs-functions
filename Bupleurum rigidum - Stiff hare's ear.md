Stiff hare's ear (_Bupleurum rigidum_) is a perennial herb of oak woodland, maquis, and garrigue across the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] fringe of [[Europe]], recorded from Catalonia, the Balearic Islands, and Valencia.[^1]
