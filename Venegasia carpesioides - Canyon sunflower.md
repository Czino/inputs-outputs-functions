Canyon sunflower (_Venegasia carpesioides_) is a large, drought-tolerant California perennial with succulent foliage and yellow, dahlia-like flowers held almost year-round, growing at the shady edge of oak woodland. It grows about 1 m tall and 0.7-1.5 m wide.[^1]
