La Purisima manzanita (_Arctostaphylos purissima_) is a low, evergreen ground-cover shrub native to the sandstone and old beach sand of California's central coast, very tolerant of drought and sandy soil.[^1] It grows 0.3-1.5 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
