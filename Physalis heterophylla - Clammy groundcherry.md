Clammy groundcherry (_Physalis heterophylla_) is a perennial herb of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] dry open woods and disturbed soils across [[North America]], its fruit edible raw or cooked; all other parts of the plant are poisonous.
