Shield pepperweed (Lepidium perfoliatum) is an annual to biennial herb of dry, sandy steppe slopes and waste ground from eastern Europe to eastern Asia, including [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe. Its spicy leaves, seeds, and young seedpods are eaten, and it is occasionally cultivated as a vegetable.
