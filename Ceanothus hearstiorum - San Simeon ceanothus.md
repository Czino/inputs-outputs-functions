San Simeon ceanothus (_Ceanothus hearstiorum_) is a flat, mat-forming evergreen shrub native to the central California coast near San Simeon, needing part shade inland and grown as a ground cover.[^1] It grows 0.07-0.3 m tall and 0.5-5 m wide.[^1]
