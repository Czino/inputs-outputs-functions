Alder buckthorn (_Frangula alnus_) is a deciduous shrub native to [[Europe|Europe]], valued for its dyes, wood, charcoal, and a long history of use as a gentle laxative. Its bark is toxic when fresh and must be dried and stored for at least a year before medicinal use.[^1]
