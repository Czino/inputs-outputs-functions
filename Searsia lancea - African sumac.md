African sumac (_Searsia lancea_) is an evergreen tree of savannahs, open woodland, and riverine rangeland across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] southern [[Africa]], its fruit fermented into beer and its bark and roots used medicinally.
