Common lilac (_Syringa vulgaris_) is a deciduous shrub native to scrub on rocky hillsides in eastern [[Europe]], widely grown for its fragrant purple, pink or white flower clusters.[^1] Its flowers are edible raw or fried as fritters, and its leaves and fruit have been used medicinally as a tonic and vermifuge.[^1] It grows up to 6 m tall.[^1]
