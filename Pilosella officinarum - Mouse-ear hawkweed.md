Mouse-ear hawkweed (_Pilosella officinarum_) is a low perennial herb of temperate and subarctic [[Europe]] to western [[Asia]] and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] It is used medicinally for respiratory problems and is a good bee and butterfly plant.[^1]
