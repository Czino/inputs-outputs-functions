Greater knapweed (_Centaurea scabiosa_) is a perennial herb of [[Europe]] and western [[Asia]] that tolerates dry, low-fertility and alkaline soils. It was an ingredient of the medieval wound salve, and is an important nectar plant for bees and butterflies.[^1]
