Broad-leaved thyme (_Thymus pulegioides_) is a low evergreen shrub of [[Europe]], from Scandinavia south to Spain, Serbia and Bulgaria. Its aromatic leaves and essential oil are antiseptic, and it tolerates dry, well-drained soils and light treading on paths.[^1]
