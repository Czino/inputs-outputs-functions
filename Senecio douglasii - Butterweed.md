Butterweed (_Senecio douglasii_, syn. _Senecio flaccidus_ var. _douglasii_) is a gray-leaved, evergreen perennial of California chaparral, coastal sage scrub, and dryland sites, with small yellow daisy flowers and useful for revegetation and bank stabilization. It grows about 1 m tall and wide.[^1]
