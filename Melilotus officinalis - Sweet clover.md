Sweet clover (_Melilotus officinalis_) is an annual or biennial herb grown as a nitrogen-fixing green manure, with edible leaves and pods and dried leaves used medicinally.

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type

- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
