Portuguese broom (_Cytisus striatus_) is a nitrogen-fixing, evergreen shrub native to Morocco, Portugal, and Spain, where it grows in dry [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] shrubland and open woodland.[^1]
