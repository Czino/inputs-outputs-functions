European dwarf cherry (_Prunus fruticosa_), also called steppe cherry, is a low, thicket-forming deciduous shrub of the Eurasian [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|steppe]] and forest-steppe, grown for its tart, cherry-like fruit.
