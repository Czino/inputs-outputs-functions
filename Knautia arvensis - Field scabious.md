Field scabious (_Knautia arvensis_) is a very cold-hardy perennial herb of [[Europe]] east to the Caucasus and western Siberia, found in meadows and pastures, usually on dry, limestone soils. It is an important nectar and pollen source for bees and butterflies.[^1]
