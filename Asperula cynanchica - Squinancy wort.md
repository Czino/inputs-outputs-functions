Squinancy wort (_Asperula cynanchica_) is a perennial herb of dry calcareous pastures and dunes across central and south-eastern [[Europe]] to the Caucasus, and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] grassland.[^2] A red dye is obtained from its root.[^1]
