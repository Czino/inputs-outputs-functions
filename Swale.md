A swale is a vegetated, open channel that slows and treats stormwater runoff for a specified water-quality volume, letting sediment settle and water infiltrate or filter through the soil.[^1] Swales work best on gentle slopes (about 1-2%); on steeper sites, small check dams along the channel can pond water and reduce flow velocity.[^1]
