Glasswort (_Salicornia europaea_) is a succulent annual herb of coastal sands, mudflats, and salt marshes across [[Europe|Europe]], temperate [[Asia|Asia]], and North Africa, with a jointed, salty-flavored stem.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Oil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Annual]]
