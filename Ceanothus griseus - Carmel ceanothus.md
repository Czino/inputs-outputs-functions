Carmel ceanothus (_Ceanothus griseus_) is a fast-growing evergreen ceanothus of the California coast, maturing into a large bush or small tree with large, glossy, dark green leaves and blue flowers, strongly favored by deer.[^1] It grows 1-3 m tall and wide.[^1]
