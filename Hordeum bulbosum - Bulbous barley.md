Bulbous barley (_Hordeum bulbosum_) is a perennial wild grass of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] dry grassland and phrygana scrub, native from S. Europe to temperate Asia and North Africa. Its grain can be ground into flour for bread or porridge, and it has been trialled as a perennial grain crop.[^1]

- [[Moist soil]][^1]
