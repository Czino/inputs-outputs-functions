Burnet rose (_Rosa spinosissima_) is a low, thorny, suckering deciduous shrub native across temperate Eurasia, from Europe to western Siberia, Mongolia, and northern Africa.[^1] It is extremely cold-hardy, tolerating temperatures down to -30°C.[^1]
