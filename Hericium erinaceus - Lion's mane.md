Lion's mane (_Hericium erinaceus_, and the related native _H. americanum_) is a forest-cultivated fungus grown on hardwood log totems, prized by chefs for a flavor said to resemble lobster or seafood.[^1]

## Inputs
- Hardwood log totems (short stacked sections), American beech and sugar maple both used successfully, inoculated with sawdust spawn the same way as shiitake.[^1]
- Spawn run: about two years before fruiting begins, longer than shiitake's one year; yield and the share of totems that fruit both peak in the third and fourth years, then decline.[^1]

- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
