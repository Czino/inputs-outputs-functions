Engelmann oak (_Quercus engelmannii_) is an evergreen oak native to [[North America|southern California]]'s foothill woodlands, its acorns leached of bitter tannins before eating and its bark gum chewed.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

- [[Acorn]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Fuel]][^1]
