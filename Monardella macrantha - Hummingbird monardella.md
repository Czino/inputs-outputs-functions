Hummingbird monardella (_Monardella macrantha_) is a low-growing, evergreen perennial native from Monterey and San Diego Counties into Baja California, its red flowers pollinated by hummingbirds. It grows 0.1-0.3 m tall and 0.3-0.5 m wide.[^1]
