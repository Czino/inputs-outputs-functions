White sweet clover (_Melilotus albus_) is a fast-growing, nitrogen-fixing legume of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and disturbed ground across [[Europe]] and [[Asia]], long used as a green manure and forage crop.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

- [[Annual]]
- [[Biennial]]

## Functions

- [[Green manure]][^1]
- [[Insect repellent]][^1]
