Siberian apricot (_Prunus sibirica_) is a cold-hardy deciduous shrub of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe and dry slopes of [[Asia|Central and East Asia]], grown for its fruit and oil-rich, almond-flavoured seed.
