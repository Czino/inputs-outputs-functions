Syrian bean-caper (_Zygophyllum fabago_) is a perennial herb of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe and semi-desert from [[Europe|south-eastern Europe]] through [[Asia|western Asia]] to Afghanistan, its pickled flower buds used as a caper substitute.
