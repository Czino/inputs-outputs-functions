Persian lilac (_Melia azedarach_), also called chinaberry, is a fast-growing deciduous tree native to [[Asia]], from N. India to China, and naturalized in the [[Mediterranean]] and other warm regions.[^1] Its cooked young leaves are eaten as a bitter pot-herb, but its fruit is poisonous and has caused fatalities, especially in children, so it should not be eaten.[^1] It grows to about 9 m tall and 9 m wide.[^1]
