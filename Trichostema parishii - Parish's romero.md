Parish's romero (_Trichostema parishii_) is a small evergreen shrub of California chaparral, preferring part shade and decomposed granite soil, more drought tolerant than woolly blue curls, with fragrant blue flowers.[^1] It grows 0.7-1 m tall and 1-1.5 m wide.[^1]
