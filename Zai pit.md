A zai pit is a planting pit dug before the rainy season and filled with compost or manure, used across dry regions to concentrate scarce rainfall and fertility at the planting point.[^1] Pits are typically spaced about 90 cm apart, 15-20 cm deep, with a mound rim about 30 cm across.[^1] The technique is traditional to the Sahel, where it is often combined with contour stone bunds and tree planting to rehabilitate degraded cropland.[^2]
