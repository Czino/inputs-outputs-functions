Couch grass (_Elytrigia repens_) is a rhizomatous perennial grass of [[Europe]], North Africa, Siberia and North America, and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^2] Its starchy rhizomes have been used as a food and coffee substitute, and its roots are a long-standing diuretic herbal remedy.[^1]
