Lotus jujube (_Ziziphus lotus_) is a spiny, deciduous shrub of dry lands and steppe across the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] regions of [[Europe]], [[Asia]] and [[Africa]], harvested from the wild for its edible fruit and for medicine. It tolerates moderate frost only after a hot, dry season.
