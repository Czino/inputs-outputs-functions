Palmer's bushmallow (_Malacothamnus palmeri_) is a narrow, evergreen, fuzzy gray-foliaged shrub endemic to openings in chaparral and mixed-evergreen forest of the Santa Lucia Mountains, San Luis Obispo County, California. It grows 1-3 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
