Quaking aspen (_Populus tremuloides_) is a fast-growing, pioneer deciduous tree of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] uplands, its inner bark ground into flour and used in traditional medicine.

- [[Hardiness zone 4]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 5]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

- [[Moist soil]][^1]
