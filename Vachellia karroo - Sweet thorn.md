Sweet thorn (_Vachellia karroo_) is a thorny, nitrogen-fixing evergreen shrub or tree of woodland, wooded grassland and riverine areas across [[Africa|southern Africa]], from the Cape north to Angola and Kenya.[^1] Its gum is eaten as a confection, its roasted seed is used as a [[Coffee substitute]], and its bark and pods yield tannins and a red dye.[^1] It typically grows to around 10 m tall, exceptionally over 20 m.[^1]
