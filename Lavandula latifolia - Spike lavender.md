Spike lavender (_Lavandula latifolia_) is an evergreen [[Mediterranean]] shrub grown for its essential oil, which it yields in greater quantity than common lavender, though of lower quality.

- [[Hardiness zone 6]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

- [[Medicinal use]]
