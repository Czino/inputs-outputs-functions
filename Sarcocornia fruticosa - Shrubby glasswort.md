Shrubby glasswort (_Sarcocornia fruticosa_) is a perennial succulent subshrub of [[Mediterranean|Mediterranean]] salt marshes and seashores, with erect, much-branched, non-rooting stems.[^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
