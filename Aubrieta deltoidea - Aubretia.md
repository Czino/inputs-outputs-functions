Aubretia (_Aubrieta deltoidea_) is a low evergreen perennial native to [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] Europe from southern Greece, growing among rocks in hilly areas to 0.2 m tall by 0.3 m wide.[^1] It has no known edible or medicinal uses, but is widely grown as a sunny ground cover.[^1]
