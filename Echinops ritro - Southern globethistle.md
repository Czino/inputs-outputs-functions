Southern globethistle (_Echinops ritro_) is a perennial herb of Central and Eastern [[Europe]] to Central [[Asia]], grown in well-drained soil of low fertility. Its root is used medicinally as an anti-inflammatory and to stimulate milk flow.[^1]
