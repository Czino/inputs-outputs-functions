Piute cypress (_Cupressus nevadensis_) is a small evergreen conifer endemic to the mountains of Kern County, California, growing fast to about 20 feet then slowly to 30 feet with gray foliage.[^1] It grows 3-10 m tall and 3-5 m wide.[^1]
