A diversion is a channel with a supporting ridge on its lower side, built across a slope to intercept and redirect surface runoff.[^1] It can divert water away from farmsteads, gullies, and other sensitive areas, protect terrace systems, or collect and direct water for storage and water-harvesting.[^1] It needs a safe, stable outlet, such as a grassed waterway or grade stabilization structure.[^1]
