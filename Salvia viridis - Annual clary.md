Annual clary (_Salvia viridis_) is an aromatic annual herb of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]], its leaves and seeds used as a flavoring and salad green and its essential oil in beer and wine.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
