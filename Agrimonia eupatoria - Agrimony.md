Agrimony (_Agrimonia eupatoria_) is a perennial herb of most of [[Europe]], north [[Africa]] and western [[Asia]], long used as a domestic herbal remedy. Its leaves, flowers and stems make a tea, and its root and leaves yield a yellow dye. Large quantities can cause digestive complaints and constipation because of its tannins.
