Magellan barberry (_Berberis microphylla_) is a spiny, evergreen shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] scrub and pasture across Patagonia in [[South America|southern Chile and Argentina]], its berries eaten fresh and its roots and stems a source of medicinal berberine and yellow dye.

- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

- [[Dye]][^1]
