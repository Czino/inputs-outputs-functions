Whiteleaf manzanita (_Arctostaphylos viscida_) is an evergreen shrub native to California, in western [[North America]], its berries edible raw or cooked and traditionally made into jelly or cider.[^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Arctostaphylos spp. - Manzanita]]
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Flammable]][^2]
