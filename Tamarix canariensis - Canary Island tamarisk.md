Canary Island tamarisk (_Tamarix canariensis_) is a deciduous tree of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] coasts and riverbanks, native to the [[Mediterranean]] basin and the Canary Islands, tolerant of saline soils.
