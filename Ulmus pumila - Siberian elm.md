Siberian elm (_Ulmus pumila_) is a fast-growing, drought-tolerant deciduous tree of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe of [[Asia|northern Asia]], widely planted as a windbreak and valued for its edible leaves, inner bark, and fruit.
