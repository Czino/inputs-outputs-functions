Water horsetail (_Equisetum fluviatile_) is a perennial herb of shallow water in lakes, ponds, and ditches across arctic and temperate [[Europe]], [[North America]], and [[Asia]]. Its roots and shoots contain thiaminase, which can deplete vitamin B1 in large quantities — heat or thorough drying destroys the enzyme.[^1]
