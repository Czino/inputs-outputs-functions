Shockley's columbine (_Aquilegia shockleyi_) is a perennial herb native to the deserts of the [[North America|southwestern United States]], its nectar-rich flowers edible raw in small quantities.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 6]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
