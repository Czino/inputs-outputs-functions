Albardine (_Lygeum spartum_) is a wind-pollinated perennial grass of dry, often gypsum-rich soils across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] interior [[Europe|Spain]] and the western Mediterranean, traditionally harvested alongside [[Stipa tenacissima - Esparto grass|esparto grass]] for its leaf fibre.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Fibre]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]
