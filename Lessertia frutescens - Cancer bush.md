Cancer bush (_Lessertia frutescens_) is a nitrogen-fixing, fast-growing deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] low-rainfall rangeland across southern [[Africa]], one of the region's most widely used medicinal herbs.

## Inputs
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Deciduous]]

## Functions

- [[Drought-tolerance]][^1]
