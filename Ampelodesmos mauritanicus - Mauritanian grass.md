Mauritanian grass (_Ampelodesmos mauritanicus_) is a large evergreen perennial grass of dry, coastal [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] habitats, with leaves traditionally used for rope- and paper-making. It grows to about 3 m tall and 1 m wide.[^1]
