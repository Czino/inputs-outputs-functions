Gray pine (_Pinus sabiniana_) is an evergreen conifer native to the dry foothills of California, in western [[North America]], its oily seeds a traditional staple food and its inner bark eaten as an emergency food.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs

- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Dye]][^1]
- [[Wood]][^1]
