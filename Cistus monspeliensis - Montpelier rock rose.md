Montpelier rock rose (_Cistus monspeliensis_) is an evergreen shrub of dry, sunny [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] scrub, with flowers and leaves used medicinally to treat wounds. It grows about 0.6-1.2 m tall and is often short-lived.[^1]

- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
