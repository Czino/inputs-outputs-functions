Riverbank grape (_Vitis riparia_) is a cold-hardy deciduous vine of riverbanks, bottomlands, and thickets across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|Eastern and Central North America]], its acidic fruit eaten fresh or dried and widely used as frost-hardy grafting rootstock.
