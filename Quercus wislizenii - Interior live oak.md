Interior live oak (_Quercus wislizenii_) is an evergreen oak native to [[North America|California]]'s interior foothills, its acorns leached of bitter tannins before being ground into flour.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Acorn]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Fuel]][^1]

## Type
- [[Quercus spp. - Oaks]]
- [[Tree]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Flammable]][^2]
