Violet sage (_Salvia nemorosa_) is a cold-hardy perennial herb of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppes, steppe slopes, and dry valley meadows, native from Spain and France east through [[Europe]] to Iran and western Siberia.[^1]
