Palmaria palmata, known as Dulse, is a red seaweed with a distinctive flavor. It is commonly used as a food ingredient in soups, salads, and snacks, and is rich in vitamins and minerals.

## Inputs
- Iron[^2]
- Potassium[^2]
- Magnesium[^2]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^2]

## Functions
- [[Calcium accumulator]][^1]
- [[Magnesium accumulator]][^1]
- [[Iron accumulator]][^1]

[^1]: [[Gaia's Garden - A Guide to Home-Scale Permaculture, 2nd Edition]] Table 6-2
[^2]: ["An Assessment of Selected Nutritional, Bioactive, Thermal and Technological Properties of Brown and Red Irish Seaweed Species"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8625894/), *Foods* 10(11):2784 (2021), which reports dulse as "one of the most widely used and consumed seaweed species in Ireland."
