Southern mule ears (_Wyethia ovata_) is a low, silver-leaved California perennial with yellow flowers, found from 1200 to 6000 feet from the southern Sierra Nevada into Baja California, and needs little care once established. It grows 0.2-0.4 m tall and 0.3-0.4 m wide.[^1]
