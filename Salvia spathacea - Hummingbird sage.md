Hummingbird sage (_Salvia spathacea_) is a low, rhizome-spreading perennial native to coastal California from Napa to Orange County, with magenta flowers heavily worked by hummingbirds. It grows 0.2-0.5 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
