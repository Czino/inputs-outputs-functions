Pigeon's scabious (_Scabiosa columbaria_) is a perennial herb of dry calcareous pastures and banks across [[Europe]] to western [[Asia]], reaching Iran and Turkmenistan, and a constituent of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] grassland.[^2] Its leaves are edible, and its flowers are a good nectar source for bees.[^1]
