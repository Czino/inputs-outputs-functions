Wild mignonette (_Reseda lutea_) is a biennial and perennial herb native to [[Europe]], including Britain, from Denmark south and east to N. Africa and W. Asia, growing in waste places, disturbed ground, and arable land on calcareous substrates.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Biennial]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Attracting bees]][^1]
