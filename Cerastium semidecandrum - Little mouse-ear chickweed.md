Little mouse-ear chickweed (_Cerastium semidecandrum_) is a small annual herb native to [[Europe]], from Scandinavia south and west to North Africa and West Asia, found on dry sandy and disturbed ground.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Annual]]
