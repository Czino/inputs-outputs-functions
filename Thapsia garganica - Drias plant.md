Drias plant (_Thapsia garganica_) is a perennial herb of rocky slopes and fields across the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]], from Iberia to Turkey and North Africa. Its root yields a resin with a long history of medicinal use, but the plant is poisonous to livestock and the resin is strongly irritant to skin.[^1]
