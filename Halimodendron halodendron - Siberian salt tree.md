Siberian salt tree (_Halimodendron halodendron_) is a nitrogen-fixing, salt-tolerant deciduous shrub native to the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe of Central Asia and the Caucasus, planted as a windbreak.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Deciduous]]

## Functions
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
- [[Windbreak]][^1]
