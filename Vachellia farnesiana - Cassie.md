Cassie (_Vachellia farnesiana_), also known as sweet acacia, is a thorny, nitrogen-fixing deciduous shrub thought to be native to tropical America and now naturalized across warm regions worldwide.[^1] Its fragrant yellow flowers are distilled into the perfume ingredient also called cassie, and its young leaflets, flowers, and green pods are edible when cooked.[^1] It grows up to 9 m tall.[^1]
