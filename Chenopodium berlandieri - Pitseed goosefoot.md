Pitseed goosefoot (_Chenopodium berlandieri_) is a fast-growing annual of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] open soils and mud-flats, its leaves and seeds a historically important wild grain and pot-herb (the raw leaves are acrid and are normally cooked before eating).
