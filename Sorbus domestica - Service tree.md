Service tree (_Sorbus domestica_) is a deciduous fruit tree native to southern [[Europe]] and the [[Mediterranean]]. Its pear-like fruit is inedible until bletted (softened by cold storage), after which it develops a sweet flavour used fresh, dried, or fermented into cider. It reaches about 20 m tall, more on favourable sites,[^2] with other sources giving 15-20 m[^3] and up to 12 m.[^4]
