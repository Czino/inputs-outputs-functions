Curlycup gumweed (_Grindelia squarrosa_) is a biennial or short-lived perennial of dry, [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairies and disturbed ground, historically used to treat bronchial and skin complaints. The plant contains the carcinogen safrole and is not used as a food.
