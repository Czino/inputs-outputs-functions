Mule's fat (_Baccharis viminea_) is an evergreen shrub native to riparian washes of [[North America|California]], its shoots roasted as a famine food only when nothing else is available, and its extensive root system valued for stabilizing sandy ground.[^1]

- [[Moist soil]][^1]

- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
