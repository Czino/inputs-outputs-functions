Red willow (_Salix laevigata_) is a deciduous tree native to riparian [[North America|California]], its sweet sap eaten like candy and its shoots and roots woven into baskets.[^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
- [[Fibre]][^1]

- [[Deciduous]]

## Functions
