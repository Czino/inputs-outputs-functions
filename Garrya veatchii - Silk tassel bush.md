Silk tassel bush (_Garrya veatchii_) is a six-foot evergreen shrub of chaparral and oak woodland from Baja California to San Luis Obispo County, with gray foliage and white catkin-like male flowers.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and wide.[^1]
