Mediterranean heath (_Erica multiflora_) is an evergreen shrub native to southern [[Europe]] (Spain, France, Italy, Croatia, Albania) and north-west Africa, part of the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] heath and garrigue flora.[^1]
