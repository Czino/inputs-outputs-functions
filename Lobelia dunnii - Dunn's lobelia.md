Dunn's lobelia (_Lobelia dunnii_) is a low, stress-deciduous perennial of wet spots in Southern California chaparral and coastal sage scrub, dying back to the ground each winter and regrowing in spring. It grows 0.1-0.5 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
