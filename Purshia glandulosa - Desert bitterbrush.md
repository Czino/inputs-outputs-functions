Desert bitterbrush (_Purshia glandulosa_) is an evergreen shrub of the Mojave Desert and eastern Sierra Nevada margins, with white rose-like flowers, browsed by wildlife through winter. It grows 1-1.5 m tall and 1.5-2 m wide.[^1]
