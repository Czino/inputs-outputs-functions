California brickellbush (_Brickellia californica_) is a shrub of dry, rocky hillsides in southwestern North America, its leaves traditionally used as a tea substitute and medicinal infusion.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]

## Functions
