Upright yellow flax (_Linum strictum_) is an annual herb of dry hills, rocky places and vineyards from central Europe across the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] to Afghanistan and Iran. Its seeds have a folk-medicinal use as an emollient.[^1]
