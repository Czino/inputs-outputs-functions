Prairie rose (_Rosa arkansana_) is a low, deciduous shrub of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairie, its hips rich in vitamin C and used fresh, dried, or in syrups, jams, and tea.
