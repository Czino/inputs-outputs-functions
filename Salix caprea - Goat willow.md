Goat willow (_Salix caprea_) is a fast-growing deciduous tree native to [[Europe|Europe]] and temperate Asia, traditionally coppiced for basketry and grown as a windbreak hedge; its bitter inner bark is a famine food eaten only when all else fails, not a regular one.[^1]
