Cleveland sage (_Salvia clevelandii_) is an aromatic evergreen shrub native to [[North America|southern California]]'s chaparral, its leaves used as a culinary sage substitute and its flowers attractive to pollinators.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
