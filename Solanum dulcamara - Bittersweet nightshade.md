Bittersweet nightshade (_Solanum dulcamara_) is a perennial climber of hedgerows and moist banks across [[Europe]], [[North Africa]], and temperate [[Asia]]. All parts of the plant, including the fruit, are mildly poisonous — adverse effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, and dilated pupils.[^1]
