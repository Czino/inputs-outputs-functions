Didier's tulip (_Tulipa gesneriana_) is a bulb of obscure origin, naturalized in south-western [[Europe]] and a parent of the cultivated garden tulips. Its bulb is edible cooked, though the bulb and flowers can cause dermatitis in sensitive people.[^1]
