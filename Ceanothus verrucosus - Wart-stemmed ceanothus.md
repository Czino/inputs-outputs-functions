Wart-stemmed ceanothus (_Ceanothus verrucosus_) is an evergreen shrub of coastal San Diego County chaparral and coastal sage scrub, named for the bumpy, rough stipules where its silver leaves meet the stem.[^1] It grows 1-2 m tall and wide.[^1]
