Plains wild indigo (_Baptisia bracteata_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial legume of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] prairies, pastures, and sandy open woods across the central and eastern [[North America|Great Plains]], used medicinally.
