Red horned poppy (_Glaucium corniculatum_) is an annual or short-lived biennial herb of southern [[Europe]],[^1] growing 0.1-0.5 m tall and wide in [[Full sun]].[^1] It contains a whitish, toxic latex sap[^2] and favors disturbed, ruderal ground such as fields and vineyards on alkaline soil.[^2]
