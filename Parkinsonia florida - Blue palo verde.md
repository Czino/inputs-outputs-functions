Blue palo verde (_Parkinsonia florida_) is a prickly, deciduous desert tree or shrub of the southwestern [[North America|North American]] deserts. Its pods and seeds are edible after thorough cooking but are mildly poisonous and can cause digestive upset in quantity, so they were traditionally eaten only occasionally, as a famine food.[^1]
