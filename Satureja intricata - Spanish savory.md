Spanish savory (_Satureja intricata_) is a small shrub native to south-eastern Spain, forming thickets among gall oaks and junipers on limestone rock from 350 to 2,100m, in the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] interior.[^1]
