Fringed rue (_Ruta chalepensis_) is an evergreen shrub of rocky, limestone ground across the [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]]. Its bitter, aromatic leaves are used sparingly as a seasoning and the plant has a long history of medicinal use, though it is an emmenagogue and abortifacient and should not be used during pregnancy.[^1]
