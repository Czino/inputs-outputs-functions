Magenta butterfly flower (_Stachys chamissonis_) is a deciduous California perennial of redwood-belt seeps and roadside drainage ditches, with purple-pink flowers, suckering freely wherever soil stays wet. It grows 0.7-1 m tall and 0.5-1 m wide.[^1]
