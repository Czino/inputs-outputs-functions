Red-heart mountain lilac (_Ceanothus spinosus_) is an evergreen shrub of California hillsides with an arborescent, tree-like habit, filling canyons with fragrant blue flowers on seasonally wet, heavy clay slopes.[^1] It grows 2-4 m tall and 2-3 m wide.[^1]
