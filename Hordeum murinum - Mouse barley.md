Mouse barley (_Hordeum murinum_) is an annual grass of disturbed ground and dry grassland across [[Europe]] and North Africa, naturalized widely as a weed. Its grain is edible but the mature awns are sharp and barbed, a handling hazard for both people and grazing animals.
