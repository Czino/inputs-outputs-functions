Curl-leaf mountain mahogany (_Cercocarpus ledifolius_) is a slow-growing, nitrogen-fixing evergreen tree of the dry, [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] interior mountain ranges of [[North America]], its extremely dense wood a traditional fuel and its bark used medicinally.

- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
