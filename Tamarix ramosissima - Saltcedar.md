Saltcedar (_Tamarix ramosissima_) is a deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] saline steppe and riverbanks across [[Asia|Central Asia]], grown as a shelter hedge and to stabilize eroding sandy soils.
