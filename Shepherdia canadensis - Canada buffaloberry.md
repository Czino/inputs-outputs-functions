Canada buffaloberry (_Shepherdia canadensis_) is a nitrogen-fixing deciduous shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] rangeland, its tart berries traditionally whipped into a foamy dessert.
