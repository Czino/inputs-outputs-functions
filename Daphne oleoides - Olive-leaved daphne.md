Olive-leaved daphne (_Daphne oleoides_) is a low evergreen shrub of southern Europe, North Africa, and West Asia to the Himalayas, growing 0.1-0.5 m tall and wide.[^2] All parts of the plant are poisonous, and its sap can irritate skin, so it is not used as a food.[^1][^2] Its roots are used as a purgative, and its bark and leaves are applied to the skin medicinally.[^1]
