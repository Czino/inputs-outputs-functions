Green ash (_Fraxinus pennsylvanica_) is a fast-growing deciduous tree of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] river valleys and plains, its inner bark cooked as food and used medicinally as a digestive bitter.
