Western aster (_Symphyotrichum ascendens_) is a rhizomatous perennial herb native to western North America, forming spreading colonies in meadows and sagebrush steppe.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
