Small-flowered tamarisk (_Tamarix parviflora_) is a deciduous shrub native to the Balkans and Aegean coasts of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]], grown as a salt-tolerant coastal shelter hedge.
