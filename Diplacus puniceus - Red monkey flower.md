Red monkey flower (_Diplacus puniceus_) is a two-foot evergreen shrub of coastal Southern California, grown as a hummingbird and butterfly plant for its red, azalea-like flowers that bloom most of spring and summer.[^1] It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 0.3-0.5 m wide.[^1]
