Foothill lupine (_Lupinus albifrons_) is a semi-deciduous, woody-based perennial shrub native to sandy and rocky sites in California and southern Oregon.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
- [[Attracting bees]][^1]
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
