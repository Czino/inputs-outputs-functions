Sweet vernal grass (_Anthoxanthum odoratum_) is a perennial grass of meadows, heaths and woodland margins across most of [[Europe]], North Africa and western [[Asia]], with a sweet hay-like fragrance from its coumarin content. A tea is made from its fresh or dried leaves, though dried plant material should be used with some caution as coumarins can act as an anticoagulant.[^1]
