The Kentucky coffee tree is a deciduous tree with large, bipinnate leaves. The seeds were historically used as a [[Coffee substitute|coffee substitute]]. The tree is also used ornamentally and in restoration projects. Although it is a member of the pea family, it does not fix nitrogen — it lacks the root-nodule rhizobia bacteria that most other legumes host.[^2][^3]
