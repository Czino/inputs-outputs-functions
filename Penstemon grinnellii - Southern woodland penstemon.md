Southern woodland penstemon (_Penstemon grinnellii_) is a drought-tolerant perennial of scattered sites in the Pinyon-Juniper and Southern Oak Woodlands of California, growing in gravel to clay soils and blooming pink-violet in late spring.[^1] It grows 0.5-0.7 m tall and 0.5 m wide.[^1]
