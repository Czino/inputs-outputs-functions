Desert wild grape (_Vitis girdiana_) is a deciduous vine native to desert washes of southwestern [[North America]], its fruit and young leaves edible and its leaves yielding a yellow dye.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Dye]][^1]

## Type
- [[Vitis spp. - Grape]]
- [[Vine]]
- [[Deciduous]]

## Functions
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
