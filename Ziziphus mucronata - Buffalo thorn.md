Buffalo thorn (_Ziziphus mucronata_) is a fast-growing, spiny deciduous shrub or tree of dry savannahs and thornveld across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and tropical [[Africa]], its fruit eaten fresh or dried and its bark, root, and leaves widely used in traditional medicine.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

- [[Tree]]
- [[Deciduous]]

- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
- [[Drought-tolerance]][^1]
