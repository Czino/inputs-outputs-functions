Serpentine manzanita (_Arctostaphylos obispoensis_) is a rare evergreen shrub, bush, or small tree native to the hills north of San Luis Obispo, California, tolerating sun or part shade and growing on serpentine, adobe, sand, or decomposed granite.[^1] It grows 1-2.5 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
