Pale dewplant (_Drosanthemum floribundum_) is a mat-forming evergreen succulent native to the Eastern Cape of [[South Africa]].[^2] It's grown as ground cover on slopes, in gravel gardens and at coastal sites,[^1] growing 0.1-0.5 m tall and spreading 1-1.5 m wide.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Perennial]]
- [[Evergreen]][^1]

## Functions
- [[Ground cover]][^1]
