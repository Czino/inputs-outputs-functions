Baby's breath (_Gypsophila paniculata_) is a perennial herb native to temperate [[Asia]] and [[Europe]], from Turkey to China, growing in dry, sandy and stony [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|steppe]] habitats alongside large tufted _Stipa_ grasses.
