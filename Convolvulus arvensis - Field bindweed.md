Field bindweed (_Convolvulus arvensis_) is a perennial climbing vine of [[Europe]] and [[Asia]], now a widespread weed of temperate regions worldwide and a constant species of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] continental steppe grassland.[^1][^2]
