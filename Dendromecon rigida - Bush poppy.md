Bush poppy (_Dendromecon rigida_) is an evergreen shrub of California chaparral with silver-grey foliage and yellow poppy flowers, growing fast on fast-draining soil and disliking fertilizer.[^1] It grows 1-3 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
