Scots pine (_Pinus sylvestris_) is a fast-growing evergreen conifer of [[Europe]] from Scandinavia south to Spain and east into temperate [[Asia]]. It grows on poor, well-drained soils, tolerates drought and wind, and yields timber, resin and turpentine.
