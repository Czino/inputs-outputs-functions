Cape aloe (_Aloe ferox_) is an evergreen succulent shrub of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] rocky hillsides and karoo margins in [[Africa|South Africa]], grown for its medicinal leaf exudate and edible flower nectar.
