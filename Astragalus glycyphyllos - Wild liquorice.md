Wild liquorice (_Astragalus glycyphyllos_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial legume of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe and grassy places across [[Europe]] to the [[Asia|Caucasus and western Asia]], its herb brewed as tea.
