Chinese milkvetch (_Astragalus sinicus_) is a nitrogen-fixing biennial legume of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] and cool-temperate fields across [[Asia|China, Japan, and Korea]], long grown as a green manure and its leaves and seed also edible.

## Inputs
- [[Moist soil]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]

## Outputs

- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
