Pitch trefoil (Bituminaria bituminosa) is a drought-tolerant, nitrogen-fixing perennial herb native to the [[Mediterranean]] basin, named for the tar-like smell of its foliage. It is used to restore soil structure on poor, degraded, or mine-waste ground.

- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
