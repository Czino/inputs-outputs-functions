Mission manzanita (_Xylococcus bicolor_) is an evergreen shrub, occasionally a small tree, native to coastal chaparral from Los Angeles to Baja California and Santa Catalina Island. It is the only species in its genus, resembling a cross between manzanita and coffeeberry, with edible fruit. It grows 1-3 m tall and 1-2 m wide.[^1]
