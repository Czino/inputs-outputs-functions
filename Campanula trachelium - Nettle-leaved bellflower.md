Nettle-leaved bellflower (_Campanula trachelium_) is a perennial herb native to [[Europe]], central [[Asia]], and the [[Mediterranean]] to Iran, found in woodlands, coppices, and hedgerows. Its common name Throatwort comes from a historical use as a sore throat remedy.[^1]
