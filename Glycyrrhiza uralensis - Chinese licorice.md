Chinese licorice (_Glycyrrhiza uralensis_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial legume native to the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe of Central Asia and Mongolia, its root a traditional sweetener and one of the most widely used herbs in Chinese medicine.
