White asparagus (_Asparagus albus_) is a spiny, scrambling evergreen perennial of hedges and scrub across the western and central [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]], from [[Europe]] to [[North Africa]]. Its young shoots are cooked and eaten, though they're considered a poor substitute for cultivated asparagus.[^1]
