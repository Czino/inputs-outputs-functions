Shepherd's needle (_Scandix pecten-veneris_) is an annual herb of arable land across [[Europe]], temperate Asia, and North Africa, from Scandinavia south to the Mediterranean. Its young stem tops are eaten raw or cooked, used in salads or as a pot-herb.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
