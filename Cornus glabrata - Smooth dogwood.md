Smooth dogwood (_Cornus glabrata_) is a deciduous shrub of stream banks and roadsides in Oregon and California, its long straight stems used in basket making.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Wood]][^1]

## Type
- [[Cornus spp. - Dogwood]]
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Deciduous]]

## Functions
- [[Flammable]][^2]
