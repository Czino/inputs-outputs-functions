Century plant (_Agave americana_) is a large evergreen succulent, naturalized across the [[Mediterranean]], whose sap is fermented into pulque and mescal and whose heart can be eaten baked. It flowers once after 7-15 years then dies; fresh sap can cause dermatitis.
