Mallow (Malva sylvestris) is a [[Fast-growing|fast-growing]] biennial or short-lived perennial common on [[Mediterranean]] waste ground, field edges, and roadsides. Its leaves, flowers, and young seeds are all edible, with the mucilaginous leaves used to thicken soups.
