Laurel sumac (_Malosma laurina_, formerly classified as _Rhus laurina_) is a large evergreen shrub of the southern California coast ranges, with shiny red-green, insect-resistant foliage and small white flowers that draw many insects in late spring. It grows 2-6 m tall and 2-7 m wide.[^1]
