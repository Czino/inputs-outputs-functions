Sage-leaved cistus (Cistus salviifolius) is a low, spreading evergreen shrub native to the [[Mediterranean]] basin, grown as a drought-tolerant ground cover on poor, sandy soils.

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Pollination]][^1]
- [[Ground cover]][^1]
- [[Drought-tolerance]][^1]
- [[Flammable]][^2]
