Alpine ragwort (_Senecio nemorensis_) is a perennial herb native to [[Europe]] and western [[Asia]], found in damp places at higher elevations. It belongs to a genus containing species with a cumulative poisonous effect on the liver, so some caution is advised with its edible and medicinal uses.[^1]
