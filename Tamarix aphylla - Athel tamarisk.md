Athel tamarisk (Tamarix aphylla) is an evergreen tree tolerant of heat, drought, and saline soils, traditionally planted as a windbreak and shelter hedge in arid regions. Its twigs exude a sweet, manna-like substance that is edible. Unlike the deciduous tamarisk species (e.g. [[Tamarix ramosissima - Saltcedar|saltcedar]]), which are highly flammable and considered invasive fire-promoting weeds across the western United States, Athel tamarisk rarely escapes cultivation and its salt- and ash-laden foliage resists burning.[^2]
