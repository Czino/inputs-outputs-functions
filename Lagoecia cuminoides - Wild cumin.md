Wild cumin (_Lagoecia cuminoides_) is a small annual herb of [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] [[Europe]] and the Near East, its seed used dried as a cumin substitute.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Type
- [[Annual]]
