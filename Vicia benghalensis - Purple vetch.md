Purple vetch (_Vicia benghalensis_) is a fast-growing annual or short-lived perennial herb grown as a nitrogen-fixing, weed-suppressing green manure, with a turquoise-green dye obtained from its flowers.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Dye]][^1]
