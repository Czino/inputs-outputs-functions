Gold of pleasure (_Camelina sativa_) is an annual oilseed crop native from eastern [[Europe]] to western [[Asia]], grown on light, well-drained soils for the edible oil pressed from its seed.

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 6]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Vegetable oil]][^1]
- [[Fibre]][^1]
