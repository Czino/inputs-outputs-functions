California barrel cactus (_Ferocactus cylindraceus_) is a solitary, barrel-shaped succulent native to the deserts of [[North America]], its seeds, fruit, and flowers edible.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Flammable]][^2]
