Hugelkultur ("mound culture") is a raised-bed technique that buries woody debris, plant material, and soil in a long mound, popularized in 1960s-80s German permaculture/biodynamic booklets. Mounds also settle and collapse as the wood decomposes, typically needing to be rebuilt within five to six years.
