Western ragweed (_Ambrosia psilostachya_) is a weedy, rhizome-spreading perennial native to dry fields and disturbed ground across California, generally not recommended for gardens and treated as an indicator of a disturbed site.[^1] It grows 0.2-1 m tall and 0.3-1 m wide.[^1]
