Western desert penstemon (_Penstemon incertus_) is a sky-blue flowered evergreen perennial of granite and sandy soils in California's Mojave Desert region, drought and heat tolerant, massing at the top of Walker Pass.[^1] It grows 0.5-1 m tall and wide.[^1]
