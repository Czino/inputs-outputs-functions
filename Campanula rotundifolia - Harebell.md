Harebell (_Campanula rotundifolia_) is a perennial herb of the northern temperate regions of the world, growing in well-drained, poor to fertile soils. Its leaves are eaten raw or cooked, and its root has been used in traditional remedies for heart and lung problems and sore eyes.[^1]
