Goat's thorn milk vetch (_Astragalus tragacantha_) is a low, thorny, nitrogen-fixing deciduous shrub of sandy and rocky coastal ground in western and southern [[Europe]] ([[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|France, Portugal and Spain]]) and [[North Africa]]. It is one of several _Astragalus_ species tapped for tragacanth gum, a mucilaginous exudate used historically as a food thickener, pharmaceutical mucilage, and textile/paper sizing agent.[^1]
