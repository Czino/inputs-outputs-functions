Littleleaf peashrub (_Caragana microphylla_) is a nitrogen-fixing, drought-tolerant deciduous shrub of sand dunes and rocky mountain slopes across the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe of [[Asia|Siberia, Mongolia, and China]].
