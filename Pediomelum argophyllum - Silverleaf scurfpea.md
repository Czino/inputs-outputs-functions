Silverleaf scurfpea (_Pediomelum argophyllum_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial legume of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] prairie and plains across the [[North America|North American]] Great Plains, its starchy root traditionally dried and ground into flour.
