Field chickweed (_Cerastium arvense_) is a low, mat-forming perennial native to [[Europe]] and temperate Asia, used in traditional medicine for injuries and uterine bleeding.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Attracting bees]][^1]
- [[Ground cover]][^1]
- [[Drought-tolerance]][^1]
