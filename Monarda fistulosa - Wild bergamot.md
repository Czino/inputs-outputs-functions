Wild bergamot (_Monarda fistulosa_) is a perennial herb of dry thickets and woodland edges across [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairie country, its aromatic leaves eaten as a potherb and brewed as tea, and long used medicinally.
