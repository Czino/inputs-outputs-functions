Jacket plum (_Pappea capensis_) is a long-lived, drought-tolerant evergreen tree of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] bushveld and rocky outcrops across eastern and southern [[Africa]], its tart fruit eaten fresh and its seed oil used for cooking and as a hair and skin remedy.
