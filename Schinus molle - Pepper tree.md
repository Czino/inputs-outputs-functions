Pepper tree (Schinus molle) is an evergreen tree native to South America, long naturalized as an ornamental and street tree across Mediterranean-climate regions including California, Spain, and Australia, with some documented invasive potential.[^2] Its dried, roasted berries are used as a peppercorn substitute ("pink pepper"), and a gum exuded from the bark is chewed.[^1]
