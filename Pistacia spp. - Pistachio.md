Pistacia is a genus of drought-tolerant trees and shrubs in the cashew family, native to warm, dry regions of the [[Mediterranean]], Middle East, and Central Asia. Individual species are valued for edible nuts ([[Pistacia vera - Pistachio|pistachio]]), resin ([[Pistacia lentiscus - Lentisk|mastic]], [[Pistacia terebinthus - Terebinth|turpentine]]), or edible seeds and oil.
