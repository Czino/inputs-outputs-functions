Safflower (_Carthamus tinctorius_) is an annual herb of dry, sunny sites, grown for its seed oil, edible young leaves and shoots, and flowers, which yield a yellow or red dye.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
