Firecracker penstemon (_Penstemon eatonii_) is a deciduous perennial of southwest desert mountains, forming a low rosette of leaves below upright stalks of showy red, tubular flowers. It grows 0.5-1 m tall and 0.5-0.7 m wide.[^1]
