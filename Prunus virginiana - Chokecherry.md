Chokecherry (_Prunus virginiana_) is a deciduous, thicket-forming shrub native to [[North America]], common across its [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] prairies, grown for its astringent dark fruit, traditionally dried and pounded into pemmican.
