Crown vetch (_Securigera varia_, syn. _Coronilla varia_) is a perennial herb native to [[Europe]] and western [[Asia]], from Spain to western Russia and Iran, growing in dry grassy places, embankments and roadsides. It spreads by vigorous rhizomes to form a ground cover, and the whole plant is toxic if eaten in quantity.
