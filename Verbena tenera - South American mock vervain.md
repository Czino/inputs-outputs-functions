South American mock vervain (Verbena tenera) is similar to [[Verbena peruviana - Peruvian mock vervain|Verbena peruviana]], providing colorful ground cover with its spreading habit and clusters of small flowers. It is drought-tolerant and used in borders, rock gardens, and containers.
