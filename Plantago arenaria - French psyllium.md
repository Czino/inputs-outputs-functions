French psyllium (_Plantago arenaria_) is an annual herb native to southern [[Europe]] and southwest Asia, cultivated for its mucilage-rich seed husks, the source of commercial psyllium.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
