Oak gooseberry (_Ribes quercetorum_) is a spiny deciduous shrub native to California oak woodland in western [[North America]], its fruit edible raw or cooked.[^1]

## Inputs
- [[Hardiness zone 7]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Ribes spp. - Currant]]
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Deciduous]]

## Functions
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
