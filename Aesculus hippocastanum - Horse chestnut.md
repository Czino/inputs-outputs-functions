Horse chestnut (Aesculus hippocastanum) is a large, fast-growing deciduous tree grown for shade and its glossy brown seeds (conkers). The roasted, leached seed has been used as a [[Coffee substitute]], and bark and seed extracts are used medicinally to support vein health.
