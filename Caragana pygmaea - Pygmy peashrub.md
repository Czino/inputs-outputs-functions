Pygmy peashrub (_Caragana pygmaea_) is a nitrogen-fixing, drought-tolerant deciduous shrub native to the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] steppe of Central Asia and Siberia, its root an emergency food.
