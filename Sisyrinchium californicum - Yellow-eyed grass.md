Yellow-eyed grass (_Sisyrinchium californicum_) is a small, iris-leaved perennial of California coast-range wetlands and coastal sage scrub, forming clumps about 8 inches tall with yellow flowers from May to June. It grows 0.2-0.8 m tall and about 0.2 m wide.[^1]
