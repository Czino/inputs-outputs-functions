Sand viper's gloss (_Echium sabulicola_) is a herbaceous plant of sandy coastal ground in the western [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]], from Spain to Italy including the Balearic Islands, Corsica, Sardinia and Sicily, and North [[Africa]].[^1] It grows up to 0.7 m tall.[^1] Sources disagree on its lifecycle: described as biennial by one source[^1] and as perennial by another.[^2]
