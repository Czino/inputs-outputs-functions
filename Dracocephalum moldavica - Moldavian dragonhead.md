Moldavian dragonhead (_Dracocephalum moldavica_), also called Moldavian balm, is a lemon-scented annual herb native to dry hills, stony riverbanks and valleys from eastern [[Europe]] to eastern [[Asia]].[^1] Its leaves make a lemon-scented tea and its young shoots are also eaten, while its essential oil, a source of citral, is used in scent-making.[^1]
