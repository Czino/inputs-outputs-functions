Black cumin (_Nigella sativa_) is an annual herb cultivated from the [[Mediterranean]] to India for its spicy, nutmeg-like seeds, used whole in cooking and pressed for oil.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
## Outputs
- [[Human food]]
- [[Medicinal use]]
