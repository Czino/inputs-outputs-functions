Smooth rupturewort (_Herniaria glabra_) is a low, mat-forming herb of dry, sandy, nutrient-poor soils across [[Europe]] and temperate Asia. Its name comes from a traditional use in treating hernias; the whole flowering plant is also used as a diuretic and expectorant.
