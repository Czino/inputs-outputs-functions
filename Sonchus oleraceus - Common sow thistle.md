Common sow thistle (_Sonchus oleraceus_) is a biennial herb whose mild-flavoured young leaves and stems are foraged and eaten raw or cooked, with medicinal uses recorded as well.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]
