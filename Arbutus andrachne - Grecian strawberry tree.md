Grecian strawberry tree (_Arbutus andrachne_) is an evergreen tree of dry, rocky [[Mediterranean climate (Csa)|Mediterranean]] scrub in southeastern Europe and western Asia, grown for its sweet, mild-flavoured fruit. It grows slowly to about 6 m tall and wide.[^1]
