Narrow-leaved coneflower (_Echinacea angustifolia_) is a cold-hardy perennial herb of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]], gravelly, dry limestone prairies across the [[North America|Great Plains]], one of the world's most widely used medicinal herbs.
