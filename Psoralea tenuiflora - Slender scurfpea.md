Slender scurfpea (_Psoralea tenuiflora_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial legume of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] dry prairies, open woods, and rocky banks across the [[North America|Great Plains]], its root edible raw, cooked, or ground into flour.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

## Type
- [[Herb]]
- [[Perennial]]

## Functions
- [[Nitrogen fixer]][^1]
- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
