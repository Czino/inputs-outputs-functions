Quail bush (_Atriplex lentiformis_) is an evergreen shrub native to the deserts of [[North America|southwestern North America]], its seeds and young leaves edible after boiling and its salt-tolerant roots valued for erosion control and windbreaks.[^1]

- [[Hardiness zone 8]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 9]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 10]][^1]
- [[Hardiness zone 11]][^1]
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]
- [[Medicinal use]][^1]

## Type
- [[Shrub]]
- [[Evergreen]]

## Functions
- [[Soil stabilization]][^1]
- [[Windbreak]][^1]
- [[Fire resistance]][^2]
