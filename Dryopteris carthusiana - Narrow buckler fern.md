Narrow buckler fern (_Dryopteris carthusiana_) is a fern native to [[Europe|Europe]], temperate [[Asia|Asia]], and [[North America|North America]], grown in damp shade for its cooked fronds and root, and its traditional use as a tapeworm remedy. The fresh plant contains thiaminase and should be thoroughly cooked or dried before use.[^1]
