Catsfoot (_Antennaria dioica_) is a low, mat-forming evergreen perennial herb of northern and central [[Europe]] to [[Asia|W. Asia]]. It spreads by stolons and has a long history of use as a folk-medicinal astringent and diuretic.[^1]
