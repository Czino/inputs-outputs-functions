Leadplant (_Amorpha canescens_) is a deciduous, nitrogen-fixing shrub of the [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairie, deep-rooted and drought-tolerant, its leaves used for tea and traditional medicine.
