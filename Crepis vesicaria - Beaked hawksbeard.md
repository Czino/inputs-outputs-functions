Beaked hawksbeard (_Crepis vesicaria_) is a biennial herb native to western and southern [[Europe]], naturalized on waysides, walls and railway banks in Britain, especially on calcareous soils.

## Inputs
- [[Full sun]][^1]
- [[Semi-shade]][^1]
- [[Well drained soil]][^1]
- [[Moist soil]][^1]

## Outputs
- [[Human food]][^1]

- [[Herb]]
- [[Biennial]]
