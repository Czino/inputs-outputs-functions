Ground elder (_Aegopodium podagraria_) is a fast-spreading perennial herb native to [[Europe|Europe]] and western [[Asia|Asia]], cultivated as a food and medicinal plant since the Middle Ages. It spreads aggressively by rhizome and is classed as a noxious weed in parts of North America outside its native range.[^1]
