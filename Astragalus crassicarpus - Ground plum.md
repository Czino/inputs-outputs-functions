Ground plum (_Astragalus crassicarpus_) is a nitrogen-fixing perennial of [[Cold semi-arid (BSk)|cold semi-arid]] [[North America|North American]] prairies and plains, grown for its fleshy, plum-like seedpods eaten raw or cooked.
