California sycamore (_Platanus racemosa_) is a fast-growing deciduous tree of creeks and foothills in California, reaching up to 75 feet, with bark chips from the trunk and roots traditionally boiled for a coffee-like drink. It grows 10-25 m tall and 8-15 m wide.[^1]
